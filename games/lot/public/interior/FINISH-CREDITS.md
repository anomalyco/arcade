# Interior finish scans

CC0 1.0, from ambientCG (https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/). Decoded offline from the read-only Ashwood texture pack; roughness and normal channels are repacked for the native renderer.

- ceiling-scan: OfficeCeiling001, https://ambientcg.com/view?id=OfficeCeiling001
- terrazzo-scan: Terrazzo019S, https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Terrazzo019S
- plaster-scan: Plaster001, https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Plaster001

Water stains, wall dirt, wax wear and tile variation are authored world-space patterns in src/interior/finishes.ts.
