# Lot

- Unlisted first-person night exterior, `/arcade/lot/`. WebGPU through `@arcade/kit`, no Three.js runtime.
- `src/interior/`: native entrance, lobby and storey-0 corridor, with physical fixtures, room-local bounce, authored finish maps and CC0 props. `scripts/import-interior.ts` executes the source builders read-only; `scripts/interior-assets.ts` decodes the five licensed prop models offline. Generated assets and their credits live in `public/interior/`. Closed door leaves and partitions are colliders; the imported slice stops at z=-3.2.
- `scripts/interior-finishes.ts` decodes the CC0 ceiling, terrazzo and plaster scans; `src/interior/finishes.ts` adds world-space wear, wall sheen and wax. Ceiling UVs align the scan's six cells with the actual 0.6 × 1.2 m T-bars. Lit-vertex aux 101–106 identifies finish recipes; glow retains its emission value. Regenerate geometry after changing importer recipes.
- `src/interior/fixtures.ts` adapts the lobby troffer to shared `SceneLight.rect`: exact Lambertian polygon diffuse, representative-point GGX and contact-hardening shadow maps. Four weighted panel samples feed the existing fog integrator. Backquote › Interior › Troffer switches Area/Point; Today and smoke use Point. The shared rectangular shader is compile-time opt-in, like the street-lamp path.
- `?view=entrance|lobby|corridor` selects a walkable matched camera. The comparison picker also includes gate and lot. `scripts/capture-interior.ts --walk` exercises pointer-lock keyboard travel through the entrance and lobby into the corridor; `--reference-only` captures the read-only Ashwood preview. Run captures headless/muted through approved HiveMind browser admission.
- Interior panes use the shared thin-glass pass; `interior/pane-uvs.ts` restores per-face affine UVs required by its derivative frame. Waxed floors carry the shared roughness tag. Inside the imported bounds, SSR uses its indoor resolution/step/streak tier with water ripples off; fog reflections also exclude indoor ripple. `scripts/compare.py --grid-only` omits exterior-only region labels when scoring interior views.
- `scripts/compare.py --lobby` measures the fixed Scrawl regions (counter, walls, ceiling, floor/chair reflections, fixture and back-wall details); its errors measure reference similarity, not visual quality. `capture-interior.ts --measure` adds paired lobby GPU timings for the 64/512-step SSR budgets after taking the still.
- `src/world.ts`: baked Ashwood exterior geometry and title camera. `src/shaders.ts`: shared physical lights, fog-sky illumination and reflected airlight. `src/settings.ts`: typed live Night presets, bound to LabPanel; shared fog and spot-shadow controls use the same panel.
- Forest: `public/ashwood/forest-models.bin.gz` holds Ashwood's 11 tree variants (LOD0/LOD1 trunk + cards) and its undergrowth clump templates; `forest-trees.bin.gz` one instance per tree (x, y, z, scale, yaw, tint, seed, variant columns). `@arcade/kit/gpu/forest` draws them (Backquote › Forest; Today reproduces the old frozen-LOD bake). Undergrowth uses the same card shading through Night uniform `N.forest`.
- Ground dressing (`src/dressing.ts`): `@arcade/kit/sim/debris` rules along the booth island kerb, booth and wagon-apron walls, props, fence base, gate apron edges and road shoulders, with lane capsules, the booth and colliders as keep-outs; heights and the road line come from the bake (`scene.json` `ground`). Backquote › Ground; Today places nothing. Rebuilds 150 ms after a setting changes.
- `scripts/capture-forest.ts [--base <main's dist>]`: headless muted stills (main, Today, new) and paired whole-frame GPU timings at the title camera.
- WASD/arrow keys, pointer-lock mouse look, Shift sprint, F flashlight, M mute, Backquote Night panel. On local/tailnet previews, P copies and opens the matching Ashwood camera on port 5253.
- Walking uses `@arcade/kit/client/walk-camera` and the quiet shared `audio/footsteps` bank. Actual displacement drives contacts and presentation; reduced motion leaves contact timing intact. Walking › Today restores the original camera and silence. `surface-query.ts` queries authored triangles; `foot-surface.ts` uses the scanned AO and shared puddle mask for wet contacts. The kit's `audio/ambience` synthesizes wind, traffic, fixture buzz, drips, HVAC and distant wildlife. `walk-audio.ts` shares the live/offline graph and suspends on focus/pointer-lock loss. Sound starts only from an explicit play gesture.
- Original-source downloads and normalization: `bun run fetch-assets`. Attribution and exact scope in `public/CREDITS.md`; mirrored source licence metadata in `public/assets/source-metadata.json`.
- `bun run check`: TypeScript, focused geometry/import tests, production build. Assets are checked in, so checks do not fetch them.
- Comparison uses Ashwood's home drift at a fixed time, FOV `68°`, near `0.15`, and `1920×1080`. The source screenshot did not record its drift clock; `START` records our chosen time. `window.lot` exposes camera, settings and frame count for automation.
- `scripts/import-ashwood.ts <afterhours-project>` reads the source checkout without modifying it. Three.js is build-time only; exported native kit buffers and the DOM-free atlas painter live in `public/ashwood/`, with source hashes in `scene.json`.
- `scripts/import-surfaces.ts` decodes the source CC0 scans and bakes material tints/roughness into PNG maps; `scripts/import-props.ts` decodes only approved/CC0 model assets and bakes the source placements. Both take the same source-project argument. `src/surfaces.ts` loads these through the shared kit adapters. Preserve the `scene.json` prop licence inventory and surface/model provenance files.
- `src/surface-materials.ts` maps ground aux values to the source road, lot, gravel and concrete wet recipes. Terrain material 42 carries grass/dirt weights in vertex colour RG; the fragment recolours forest-floor luminance. Keep the exporter and shader tags together when regenerating geometry.
- `scripts/capture-detail.ts` captures five acknowledged 1920×1080 camera pairs from the existing previews. `scripts/ground-crops.py` splits the fixed page captures and writes road, lot and booth ground crops. Reject runs with browser errors or incomplete poses; these comparisons include lighting differences, so their pixel error is not a material-only metric.
- Facility stair glow needs at least 7 cm clearance from its opaque backing at the distant title camera; the source's 5 mm gap assumed transparent glass. Do not move the glow out over the mullions.
- `scripts/capture.ts` runs headless and muted through HiveMind. `scripts/compare.py` records display-RGB error, linear luminance, hue, histograms and an 8×6 grid against the saved reference, plus side-by-side and difference images.
- `public/compare.html` is the local-preview camera bridge: Lot on 5251 drives Ashwood's local-only debug hook on 5253. The page is also served at tailnet HTTPS 5254. `scripts/prove-compare.ts` checks actual applied cameras and pointer-lock input; `scripts/prove-serving.ts` checks the real Worker asset paths and decoded mesh bytes.
- `src/mesh-bytes.ts` detects gzip magic after Fetch: both raw `.gz` assets and servers that set `Content-Encoding: gzip` are valid. Assets live under `dist/arcade/lot`, so the Worker preserves the URL prefix.
- Normal-map mip length feeds roughness variance; physical AA uses the mapped normal. Wet reflections run after scene TAA with their own history, four deterministic lobe samples and a tunable radiance cap. Keep wet-region edges footprint-filtered.
- The road's wet term (puddles, film) is the kit's `gpu/reflections/wet` port of Ashwood's `wet()`, and the asphalt tags its roughness in HDR alpha. Kit SSR (`gpu/reflections/ssr`, Reflections group, Today turns it off) runs as a volume slot before the fog slot and adds mirrored geometry; FogScatter keeps the analytical emitter/sky reflections. FogScatter takes raw emitter radiance (no diffuse BRDF division by π), including the booth bulb. Flashlight uses the shared Outbreak beam profile and is unshadowed. Practical lights share three perspective shadow slots, including the booth ceiling light; cutout foliage is not a shadow caster. Static imported mesh adapter does not implement skins, animations, clearcoat or emissive maps.
- Glass panel (Night › Glass): thin glass on the booth (`@arcade/kit/gpu/materials/glass`, a blended pass over the `glass` stream) and interior-mapped rooms behind the facility's lit windows (`windows` stream, baked 3 cm proud of Ashwood's window proxies by `import-ashwood.ts`; `aux = kind·4 + brightness`, dark bays get brightness 0). Shadows › Contact shadows is a half-res depth volume slot (`@arcade/kit/gpu/contact-shadows`). All three are on in normal play and Today under `?smoke`, where neither pass nor the room atlas is built. `scripts/glass-proof.ts` takes stills and paired GPU cost through HiveMind.
- Glow decals (MAT glow, interior-window panes) carry a depth offset in 24-bit steps (`SceneHooks.depthBias`, Glass › Glow decal depth offset): the stairwell glow is 5 mm off its backing, under one depth step at 150 m. `scripts/motion.ts` measures temporal stability on scripted walk/strafe/turn paths (`lot.step` renders synchronously on a synthetic 60 fps clock; per-pass toggles; reprojected per-pixel change, pops and world-anchored ROIs).
- Street lamps (`src/lamps.ts`, kit `gpu/lights/street`): sodium heads with a pole collider are lens emitters with roadway photometry, lens airlight and PCSS from the lens (Backquote › Lamps; Today restores the round point spot). The lens bowls are a level chunk of material 41, emissive in `shaders.ts`. `scripts/capture-lamps.ts` captures the lamp cameras (and Ashwood at the same pose) and `--measure` pairs Today vs lens GPU time.
- Weather (Backquote › Weather; `?weather=drizzle|rain|storm` deep link, `lot.setWeather`, `lot.strike(plan?)`): `src/weather/` is a lazily imported chunk, so Today and `?smoke` never fetch, compile or draw it. Rain is the kit `WeatherRenderer` (lamp-lit streaks, splashes, eave drips, mist; reactive mask under TAA) fed the nearest ten practicals; `shelter.ts` is the one list of sheltered rects and dripping eaves for rain, ground and sound. Lightning is the kit's `weather/lightning` (seeded stepped-leader walk + midpoint displacement, Rakov & Uman stroke timing, the WCAG 2.3.1 `FlashLimiter`) drawn by `weather/lightning-pass` (additive HDR ribbons, RGB-only so the HDR alpha roughness tag survives); one scene-light pulse per strike lights the lot through Night `N.storm` (`lotFlash`, `lotSkyFlash`) and the fog (brightness, blue hue, cloud deck, history 1 while lit). Lightning flashes Full/Reduced/Off; Reduced is the default under prefers-reduced-motion. Sound is `weather/audio.ts` inside the walking master: library ids `weather.*` (ElevenLabs, Anomaly licence), positional beds, drips, gusts, thunder at distance/343 with facade echoes and a brown-noise roll, behind a limiter. `scripts/lot-storm-proof.ts stills|strike|walk|measure|today` (root `scripts/`) takes the proofs headless and muted.
