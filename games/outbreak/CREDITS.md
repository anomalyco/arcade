# Outbreak shared-sky credits

The opt-in night sky uses the Yale Bright Star Catalogue BSC5, public-domain JSON conversion by aduboisforge,
and NASA SVS CGI Moon Kit 2019 by Ernie Wright / LRO–LROC. The colour map is compressed and mip-filtered;
the catalogue is normalized into a binary asset.

Exact sources, licence evidence and hashes: [shared sky credits](../../packages/kit/sky/assets/CREDITS.md) and
[provenance](../../packages/kit/sky/assets/sources.json).

## Textured furniture

The optional rotary phone, TV and kitchen-counter GLBs use original OpenAI-generated product shots, TRELLIS.2
MIT code/weights with BiRefNet cutouts, and Blender Cycles high-to-low PBR transfer. Source/asset hashes,
changes and the offline DINOv3 licensing caveat are in
[shared prop credits](../../packages/kit/props/assets/CREDITS.md) and
[provenance](../../packages/kit/props/assets/textured-sources.json), also served under `prop-assets/`.
