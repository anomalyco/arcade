# Name research: ASHWOOD

Checked 2026-10-19 with web search, fetch and the Steam store search and appdetails endpoints. This is not legal
advice. I could not query the official USPTO search or EUIPO eSearch directly because both need JavaScript, so the
trademark findings come from third-party indexes of USPTO data (Furm, Trademarkia, TrademarkElite, Usmarkdb). Run a
proper clearance search before you register anything or spend money on the name.

Use: an indie first-person horror game set in 1984 at a US government radio station, "Ashwood Outpost", in a foggy
forest. ASHWOOD would be the game title and the station name.

## Verdict

- **Game title "ASHWOOD": avoid.** The trademark picture is clean, but the setting is already taken in games:
  - **Radiolight** (Kryštof Knesl, published by Iceberg Interactive, Oct 2025) is set in **Ashwood Creek, 1985**. It
    is a first-person game where you follow **mysterious radio signals** into the **Ashwood Creek National Park**
    forest. It has 432 Steam reviews (Very Positive, 90%), a Wikipedia article, an Epic Games Store listing and press
    coverage. Its store copy repeats "Ashwood Creek" again and again.

    An ASHWOOD game about a 1984 radio station in a foggy forest has the same name, decade, setting and core mechanic
    (radio signals in the woods). Players and press will read it as a sequel, a spin-off or a copy.
  - **Midnight At Camp Ashwood** (Palfrey Games, Steam, Apr 2026) is a first-person psychological horror game in a
    "dense, fog-filled forest" with audio diaries. It overlaps on title, genre, perspective, fog and forest.
  - There are at least five more horror uses of "Ashwood" on Steam, itch.io and Roblox (see section 1), plus a cozy
    console game, *Ashwood Valley*, and two small studios called Ashwood Games.
- **Station name inside the game ("Ashwood Outpost"): risky.** It is legally low risk as a fictional place name, but
  the Radiolight overlap (1980s, forest, radio) remains even when it is just a place name. Players who know
  Radiolight will notice.

## 2. Trademarks

US data from third-party indexes:

- **No live US ASHWOOD word mark for software or video games (class 9) turned up.** No ASHWOOD filing in class 9
  appeared at all.
- **Class 41:** the only related hit is **ASHWOOD PRESS** for book publishing, which is registered (two filings, Feb
  2023, serials 97779409 and 97779390). It covers publishing rather than games, but it sits in class 41.
- **Other ASHWOOD marks are in unrelated classes:**
  - Live: addiction treatment (class 44, Northpoint Recovery, Reg. 5568595, incontestable)
  - Live: hard root beer (2021)
  - Live: ASHWOOD ROAD furniture
  - Live: ASHWOOD'S honeycombs
  - Live: ASHWOOD ADVISORS (finance)
  - Abandoned: hotels and bars (2023), cutting boards, supplements, clothing, wine

  None of these blocks a game, but they show the word is a common brand.
- Neither Radiolight nor Ashwood Games appears to have registered "Ashwood". Radiolight's mark is "Radiolight", and
  "Ashwood Creek" is only its setting. The risk is confusion and reputation, not an infringement claim.
- I could not check EUIPO or UK filings. Radiolight's developer is Czech, its publisher is Dutch and Ashwood Games is
  UK-based, so check EUIPO eSearch, TMview and the UK IPO for "ASHWOOD" in classes 9, 28, 41 and 42.

## 3. Film, TV, books and podcasts

"Ashwood" is a popular fictional horror place name, so there is no single dominant work but a lot of small ones:

- **Books:**
  - *Ashwood* by C.J. Malarsky (2015, YA horror; a girl is trapped in nightmares of Ashwood Asylum; 117 Goodreads
    ratings).
  - *Ashwood* by Logan Myers (Aug 2025, 448–500 pages). It is set in Ashwood, Arkansas, in summer 1987, among Ozark
    forests: "Twin Peaks and Stranger Things" small-town supernatural horror with a hum in the woods and missing
    children. It is close in tone and decade.
  - *Ashwood* by P.E. Mather (Austin Macauley, a ghost story about the Ashwood Farmhouse).
  - *Ashwood* by Alina Lee (2021, gothic occult horror set at Ashwood Manor).
  - Eva Ashwood, a popular dark-romance author (about 940k Goodreads ratings), owns a lot of the bookstore search
    results for "Ashwood".
- **Podcasts and audio:**
  - *Echoes Under Ashwood* (launched Apr 2026), a cosmic-horror audio drama about Gen-X friends whose childhood in
    Ashwood, Ohio, hid disappearances. It has an '80s nostalgia hook.
  - *Ashwood*, a narrated horror anthology podcast (audio-drama.com).
  - *ASHWOOD*, YouTube horror shorts and audio drama (Oct 2026).
- **Film and TV:**
  - *Dishonest* (2019, UK short action-horror set in "Ashwood Forest", a town with Twin Peaks influences; screened
    at Horror-on-Sea).
  - Storm Ashwood, an Australian horror director (*The School*, 2018).

  No feature film or TV series titled *Ashwood* was found. ashwoodseries.com is a WordPress theme demo, not a real
  show.

## 4. Places and brands

- **Places:** there is no dominant real "Ashwood". The biggest is Ashwood, Victoria, a Melbourne suburb (population
  about 7,000), followed by small US communities in Oregon (a ghost town), Tennessee, South Carolina, Texas and
  Virginia, and a village in Staffordshire. None is a military or government site, and none carries strong
  connotations.
- **Ashwood University:** a well-known Pakistani diploma mill that sold "life experience" degrees (it has a Wikipedia
  article and appears in fake-degree scandals). It is a mildly negative association, but it is niche.
- **Brands:** the name is used by many small companies (rehab centres, financial advisers, furniture, a London
  company called Ashwood Studios Ltd, which provides business support rather than games). No single brand dominates.

## 5. Search crowding

- **"Ashwood game":** results are led by **Radiolight** (Steam, its official site, Wikipedia), then *Ashwood
  Conspiracy*, *Midnight At Camp Ashwood*, the Ashwood Games studio pages, *Ashwood Valley* on console, and Roblox's
  *Ashwood: SURVIVAL*. That is crowded, and the leader is the closest match to your premise.
- **"Ashwood horror":** Radiolight first again, then *Midnight At Camp Ashwood*, the Roblox game, *Ashwood Murder*,
  *The Last Portrait* (Ashwood Manor), several *Ashwood* horror novels, two horror podcasts and a YouTube horror
  series. That is very crowded for a niche word.
- **Steam store search "ashwood":** 18 results. The first six are *Ashwood Conspiracy* (plus its demo), *Midnight At
  Camp Ashwood*, *The Last Route Together – Ashwood Trip* and *Radiolight* (plus its demo).
- **"Ashwood" alone:** a mix of the Melbourne suburb, the diploma mill, Eva Ashwood, local businesses and the games
  above. A new game would not rank without a subtitle, and with one it still competes with Radiolight for the exact
  "1980s Ashwood forest radio" idea.

## Next steps

1. Drop ASHWOOD as the title. If the "ash" sound is wanted, check variants with less baggage, but avoid any
   "Ash-" + "wood/creek/grove" compound because of Radiolight.
2. The clearer candidates from the NORTHWOOD report (**Whitebark**, **Larchwood**) remain options. Re-check them
   against recent releases before choosing.
3. Before registering any name, search USPTO (classes 9, 28 and 41) and EUIPO/TMview.
