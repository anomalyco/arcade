# Name research: NORTHWOOD

Checked 2026-10-19 with web search and fetch. This is not legal advice. I could not query the official USPTO
search or EUIPO eSearch directly because both need JavaScript, so the trademark findings come from third-party
indexes of USPTO data (Furm, Markinton, Trademarkia). Run a proper clearance search before you register anything or
spend money on the name.

Use: an indie first-person horror game set in 1984 at a US government radio research station in a foggy pine
forest. NORTHWOOD would be the game title or the station name ("Northwood Station").

## Verdict

- **Game title "NORTHWOOD": avoid.** In gaming, and especially in horror, "Northwood" already means Northwood
  Studios, the developer and publisher of *SCP: Secret Laboratory*:
  - It is a free multiplayer first-person horror game about a government-style containment facility.
  - It has about 92,000 Steam reviews, rated Very Positive.
  - Its official YouTube channel is called "Northwood".
  - The studio styles itself "Northwood Studios™" on Steam.

  A horror game named NORTHWOOD would show up beside, and be read as connected to, the best-known "Northwood" in
  the genre. The core idea is also close: a secret facility where staff died and something got out.
- **Station name inside the game ("Northwood Station"): risky but usable.** Trademark risk for a fictional place
  name in the story is low. However, players and press will still link it to SCP, the name gives you nothing for
  search, and it will compete with Northwood Studios, Northwood University, Northwood HQ in the UK and about 50
  real towns. "Operation Northwoods" (1962) adds an unintended US-government false-flag connotation.

## 2. Trademarks

US data from third-party indexes:

- **No live US NORTHWOOD word mark for video games or downloadable game software (class 9) turned up.** The only
  class 9 hit was NORTHWOOD for GIS and mapping software, filed by Northwood Geoscience in 1999 and abandoned in
  2002.
- **Class 41:** Northwood University holds live registrations, including Reg. 3670531 for classes 41 and 25 and
  "NORTHWOOD UNIVERSITY TIMBERWOLVES" for university sporting events. These cover education and sports, not games.
  "NORTHWOOD RETAIL" for social entertainment events is pending (2024).
- **Other live NORTHWOOD marks are in unrelated classes:**
  - ammunition and guns (class 13, 2019)
  - RVs (class 12)
  - industrial machinery
  - carpeting
  - cigars (pending 2026)
  - sauna goods

  These don't matter for a game, but they show the word is widely used as a brand.
- **Northwood Studios:** it uses "™", which means it claims the mark without necessarily having registered it. I
  couldn't confirm a USPTO or EUIPO registration. Even unregistered, it has common-law and passing-off rights in
  games through long, heavy use in the EU and US. A registration could also be pending in the EU or Poland, so check
  EUIPO eSearch and TMview for "NORTHWOOD" in classes 9, 28, 41 and 42.

## 3. Other strong associations

- **Northwood Headquarters (UK).** This is the UK's main military command site, in Eastbury, Hertfordshire, next to
  Northwood, London. It houses:
  - the Permanent Joint Headquarters (PJHQ)
  - Cyber & Specialist Operations Command
  - NATO Allied Maritime Command
  - the Royal Navy Maritime Operations Centre

  In British news "Northwood" means military command. That fits the government tone, but it is a real, active
  military headquarters, and UK search results will be full of it.
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwood_Headquarters
  - https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/the-permanent-joint-headquarters
- **Operation Northwoods (1962).** A US Joint Chiefs proposal for false-flag attacks to justify invading Cuba. It is
  one letter off and is a staple of conspiracy theories. For a story about a secret US government station, many
  players will assume the reference is deliberate.
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Northwoods
  - https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/CMC-60/joint-chiefs-pretexts-to-invade-Cuba-1962
- **Northwood University.** A business school in Midland, Michigan, with NCAA Division II teams called the
  Timberwolves. It owns live US marks.
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwood_University
- **Places.** About 50 US communities and about 10 in the UK are named Northwood, including towns in New
  Hampshire, Iowa, North Dakota and Ohio, a Baltimore neighbourhood and Northwood, London (with its Tube station).
  None is dominant, but together they dilute search results.
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwood
  - https://roadsidethoughts.com/nh/northwood-xx-rockingham-profile.htm
- **Film, TV and books:**
  - *Northwood*, a Canadian CBC teen drama (1991–94).
  - *Northwood* (2014), a found-footage crime and horror film.
  - *Northwood*, a horror-tinged literary novella by Maryse Meijer (2018), set in a forest.
  - *Northwoods* (2016), a Bill Schweigart monster-horror novel set in the Minnesota woods.
  - *The Northwoods Chronicles*, a supernatural novel by Elizabeth Engstrom.
  - *The Northwood Project*, a 2024–25 horror audio drama about disappearances in a small town called Northwood.

  "The Northwoods" is also the common name for the forests of the Upper Midwest, used for many regional brands.
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwood_(TV_series)
  - https://foundfootagecritic.com/northwood-2014/
  - https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/673535/northwood-by-maryse-meijer/
  - https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/532540/northwoods-by-bill-schweigart/
  - https://www.risingshadow.net/book/32889-the-northwoods-chronicles
  - https://audiofiction.co.uk/show.php?id=20240206-03

## 4. Search crowding

- **"Northwood game":** results are dominated by *SCP: Secret Laboratory* and Northwood Studios (Steam store and
  publisher pages, Wikipedia, the SCP:SL wiki, LinkedIn, GitHub, EULA pages, Roblox SCP clones naming "Northwood
  Studios"). Next come small itch.io studios and titles named Northwood. A new game would be competing with a
  long-running free-to-play game that has a big community and a lot of backlinks.
- **"Northwood horror":** again led by SCP:SL. After that come the films, the novella, the Schweigart novel,
  *The Northwood Forest* and the audio drama.
- **"Northwood" alone:** results are mixed across the UK military HQ, Northwood University, Northwood, London,
  real-estate and other businesses, and SCP:SL. A game would not rank without a subtitle.
- **Steam store search:** "northwood" returns Northwood Studios first.
