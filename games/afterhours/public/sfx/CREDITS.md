`eleven_text_to_sound_v2` (the source takes and their prompts are in `audio-lab/`), under Anomaly's commercial
ElevenLabs plan. No attribution is required; ownership of the output stays with us (ElevenLabs Terms §4(c)(ii)).

2026-10-09): the sounds may be used only combined with other material, here inside the Afterhours game. They are
not covered by any licence of this repository and may not be distributed on a standalone basis, as isolated audio
files or samples, or as part of any sound, sample, effects or music library. The same applies to the source takes
in `audio-lab/sfx/` and `audio-lab/sfx-raw/`.
