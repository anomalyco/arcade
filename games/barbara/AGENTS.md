# BARBARA (formerly Woods)

- Unlisted co-op game at https://anoma.ly/arcade/barbara/ (Worker `arcade-barbara`): solo with bots, or a room of 2–6 shared by link (`?room=<code>`). Keep `noindex`, no landing panel, listed in the README's prototypes. Kit shipped it on Oct 22 2026 as BARBARA: "public for everyone, but if you know the link… Creature on, realistic people, all the cool fixes." Concepts 1 (flashlights on a forest trail), 2 (fog survey with compass, map and flagging tape) and 4 (summer camp '87, the spawn and base) are one game; the story is Find Barbara (`STORY.md`).
- Work goes to `main` by PR like every game; the `woods` branch is retired. Internal names stay "woods" where renaming would be churn (`window.woods`, `WoodsState`, `stepWoods`, `@arcade/kit/forest`, the Lab's `woods.*` setting keys).
- Defaults Kit set at the ship: realistic friends on (blocky one setting away), the creature on (rooms too; since Oct 22 the figure and the spider, `creature: both`), the catalogue night sky on (`skyMode: "physical"` in `src/world-view.ts`; Today one setting away; the software-GPU smoke keeps Today and blocky friends), the highlight shoulder on, camera looks off, Today looks one setting away.
- Phase 2 (rooms) is built on `@arcade/kit/server` (Archers' pattern: kit `RoomCore` + `RoomObject` + `LobbyObject`).
- Camp detail is on by default in `src/world-view.ts` (Kit's round 8–11 approval): grounded steps, firewood, matte paths and the cold stone ring. `woods.campDetail` stays a reload setting for Looks presets; `WorldView.setWorld` rebuilds its geometry. Shared kit default is off. Surface lights pack game sources before bounce and camp, including under the 64-light cap (`packages/kit/forest/lights.test.ts`).
- Deploy recovery: `WoodsRoom` stores a private version-1 SQLite checkpoint every 2 s and on seat changes. It contains the whole authoritative night, world parameters and private seat credentials; restored sockets start with empty input queues and keep the original 90 s grace deadline. Checkpoints carry `BUILD` (the shared simulation/world source hash): corrupt or incompatible saves are logged and discarded, so rendering-only deploys preserve a night while shared-code changes start fresh. Pencil paths and copies use centimetre precision (six fully saturated maps: ~1.05 MB); write failures back off from 2 s to 60 s without stopping play. A build mismatch reloads the same `?room=` up to five times, with 5/10/20/40 s waits, then asks for a manual reload; a welcome resets that room's retry count. Full join snapshots reset prediction/map cursors and restore camera facing. `scripts/restart-proof.ts` runs two production `OnlineSession`s against local Wrangler, restarts workerd, then rolls an incompatible build while serving stale assets through two reloads. Run with HiveMind browser; output is `captures/barbara-restart/restart.json`.
- Physical Space jumps: the same `sim/hop` ballistic hop as Night Shift, 0.3 m, shared by room/solo/prediction; one-shot inputs survive jitter-buffer stalls. Camera height and both friend renderers follow it. `Walk.land` supplies the quiet two-foot landing, with a 35 mm camera dip (off with reduced motion). All walkers, bots included, have solid 0.28 m plan-radius bodies: `stepMotion` passes peers to `stepBody` for stop/slide without pushing. Online prediction uses latest authoritative peer positions, not the delayed drawing poses. `tests/playtest-net.test.ts` and `tests/jump-camera.test.ts` cover persistence, collision and hop/landing behavior.

## Layout

- `shared/world.ts`: the forest as plain data from `WorldParams` (seed, half size, trunk density, marker count). Terrain height (`terrainHeight`/`groundHeight`, `standHeight` adds the dock), lake, camp buildings and place lights, the old path, trees, rocks, survey markers, the ridge mast light, a tree grid for reach and collisions (`treesNear`, `solidsNear`). No trig or `Math.random` in generation, so a server builds the same world.
- `shared/sim.ts`: `WoodsState` (players, bot brains, tapes, found markers, map knowledge per player, `rev` counters, the distant-sound schedule) and `stepWoods(state, world, inputs, dt, feel)`, authoritative in the room and run locally solo. `stepMotion` (look, map raise, `movePlayer`) is the one walking step shared by the room, solo and client prediction. `PlayerInput` is `{ yaw, pitch, light, tie, map, mx, mz, sprint, flare }`. Ids: solo you are 0, room people 1–6, bots from `BOT_ID` (100); bots follow the nearest person and answer any signaller. `FEEL` is every feel constant; its knobs are `src/feel-controls.ts` (kept out of `shared/` so the server has no DOM types).
- `shared/night.ts`: one night as a game (rules and numbers in `NIGHT.md`): STORY.md's five key moments as seeded clues (car, page, badge, tape recorder, the extra searcher's strip by the red ridge light) each pointing to the next, the verdict at the radio by the tower (`shared/verdict.ts`: suspects, seeded culprit and evidence, votes, a wrong name taking searchers; `src/verdict-hud.ts` is the naming card), the goal line (`nextStep`, `#goal`), tape coverage in the debrief, clues `EDGE_MARGIN` inside the edge, dawn after `FEEL.dawnMinutes`, torch batteries and spares, win and lose, the debrief, bots working leads (`botWaypoint`), and the spider's hooks (`Player.isolated`, `Player.capture`). `src/night-view.ts` draws the clues and spares and greys the sky (`DawnSky`); `src/night-hud.ts` is the end card and debrief; the held map pencils clues in red (`pencil.ts`) and the watch on the left wrist tells the time (`props.ts` `watchFrame`, hands as their own objects). `tests/night.test.ts` plays bot nights to a win and to losses; `scripts/night-sweep.ts` measures bot nights over many seeds.
- `shared/protocol.ts`: wire format (JSON). 30 Hz room, 15 Hz snapshots; inputs quantized (`packIntent`) before the client predicts with them. Snapshots carry compact player rows, your authoritative motion and ack (`you`, `ack`), finds and names when they change, and your map as a `MapDelta` (new path points, tape ids learned/gone, copied paths' appended points; `applyMapDelta` on the client). The tape list comes once on join; `tape-tied`/`tape-removed` events keep it after. Everything a room holds is bounded: bots keep `botTapes` up each and only tie bare trunks, `MAX_TAPES` in all, removed tapes leave every map, copies of a leaver's path are dropped, and paths are append-only with spacing that widens every 500 samples (`TRAIL_MAX` 2000). `scripts/bandwidth-hour.ts` measures a simulated hour (6 people: ~4.0 KB/s per seat, flat). `TEAM` 4 (bots fill up to it), `GRACE_MS` 90 s seat hold, `ROOM_FEEL` = `FEEL` (Lab walking knobs don't change the room).
- `shared/distant.ts`: the distant-sound schedule (see Sound); `shared/voices.ts`: the searchers' voices (see Story and voices). `stepWoods` steps it at the tick rate with the people (not bots) and pushes `DistantEvent`s (`kind: "distant"`) into `state.events`; rooms broadcast them like any event and send `DistantState` once on join (`Snapshot.distant`). Clients only play events.
- `shared/creature.ts`: the thing in the woods (Kit's concept 2, "distant and brief"), on by default (`FEEL.creature`; a room runs the defaults, so rooms have it too). A seeded stream inside `DistantState.creature` (its own rng, so the sounds' schedule is unchanged), stepped in `stepWoods` with every walker (bots carry beams) and broadcast as a `CreatureEvent` (`at`, plan path `x0 z0 x1 z1`, `dur`, `seed`). Never before 4–6 min, then about every 5 min ÷ `creatureRate`, and only when a person looks (±40°) towards something bright 49+ m away in clear sight: a friend's signal, a friend's level beam aimed back at them (within 35°), or a lit camp window or porch with them outside camp. A beam across the view doesn't count, and with none of these nothing comes: a black figure reads only where it covers light. It crosses that line 45–80 m out (`creatureNear`/`creatureFar`), in front of the light (biased to the near edge and to farther lights, so it cuts out more of it), at `creatureSpeed` (11 m/s) for 0.5–1.2 s, never within 35 m of any person, preferring ends hidden by trunks. It waits for a distant sound to finish and keeps the next one 6 s off it, but ignores the signal hold (signals are its best backdrop). `src/creature-view.ts` draws it: procedural thin limbs on the kit rig's `limb`/`emit`, every part unlit black (`MAT.glow`, no glow) and depth-writing, so the fog march stops at it and it reads only against lit mist; its batches go into the TAA reactive mask. `Soundscape.creature` synthesizes a brush crash (`brushCrash`) that moves along the path with travel delay, distance filtering and reverb, a few dB under a far branch snap, and hushes the crickets briefly. Lab › Creature: On, how often, distance from/to, speed, Trigger now (solo: in the sim; with no bright light out there it stages a friend's signal 64–96 m ahead and triggers a second later; a room: this machine only). `scripts/creature-visibility.ts` scores it from rendered frames (black, white-mask and bare passes on one clock; region means over the covered lit pixels, since the sensor grain re-rolls each pixel every frame; reads at ≥ 12 lit px, drop ≥ 0.02, Weber ≥ 0.25 for 3+ frames) and writes stills and clips per backdrop. Measured 2026-10-22 (peak Weber, rough): signal 45 m 0.39, 80 m 0.48; aimed back 45 m 0.34, 60 m 0.23, 80 m 0.30; camp windows 45 m 0.22, 60 m 0.52; sideways beam 60 m read in one frame only. Even where it reads, it's for 0.1–0.6 s of the run, while it passes the bright core. `scripts/creature-proof.ts` records the earlier clip and stills (headless, muted, HiveMind).
- `shared/flares.ts`: road flares (1996 fusees), stepped in `stepWoods`. Each person carries one (`Player.flares`), the camp's picnic table has `FLARE_SPARES` (2), taken within `FLARE_PICKUP` (1.9 m) with an empty pocket; bots never strike theirs. Someone taken (`Player.capture`) lets a held flare fall where they stand (`dropFlare`, `flare-dropped`) and strikes nothing. Aim uses `sinApprox`/`cosApprox`. Hold `KeyQ` (input bit 32; 16 is jump): the cap scrapes, catches `FLARE_SCRAPE` (0.35 s) later (`Flare.lit`) and burns in the hand; letting go, no sooner than `FLARE_BEAT` (0.55 s) after it caught, throws it at `FLARE_SPEED` (10.5 m/s) a little above the look. A tap does all of it. The room rounds the launch to mm and sends it; the flight is `@arcade/kit/sim/thrown` (fixed 1/120 s substeps, + − × ÷ √, bounces off trunks, rocks, walls and roofs via `flareEnv`, floats on the lake), so every client flies the same arc and lands it where the room did, and the rounded resting place is sent too (`flareRow`/`applyFlares`, `Snapshot.fl` on `flares.rev` change; your pocket count is `you[10]`; flares are in the deploy checkpoint, and a restored seat's key counts as up). It burns `FEEL.flareBurn` (150 s, room-authoritative), level from `@arcade/kit/sim/flare-burn`. `flareSources` is every lit flare as a light (held ones in the holder's hand): the creature's backdrops (`Beacon`, a held flare belongs to its holder) and what a spider or anything else in the sim can read. Maps within `FLARE_SEEN` (150 m) of one burning on the ground mark it (`MapKnowledge.flares`, copied on map compare, a red crayon asterisk in `pencil.ts`). `layFlare` lays a burning one for automation and tests (`woods.layFlare(x, z, age)`, `woods.throwFlare()`). `tests/flares.test.ts`.
- `src/flare-view.ts` `FlareView`: each flare's kit `FlareFx` (flame, sparks, slag, smoke in one `Particles` pool lit only by the flares, drifting with `gustAt`), its scene light (`FLARE_LOOK`: power 4, reach 18 m; trunk polar shadows from `WorldView.occluders` via `ForestView.setOccluders`, nearest 3/2/1 flares on High/Balanced/Low), a mist fog lamp within 70 m, a far point glow past 8 m, a soft far haze past 40 m (`ForestGlow.soft`) that trunks cut up, and the sticks (in hands, flying, lying, the spares on the table). Your own held flare is drawn low right at arm's length with its mist glow, smoke and halo cut. Lab › Road flare: Flame and smoke (kit `FlareSettings`), Light, Throw a flare (`woods.trigger.flare`). `scripts/flare-proof.ts`: stills (hand, 5/30/60/120 m, a 120 m gap line, none), paired GPU and offline sound levels (headless, muted, HiveMind browser; `--stills` for stills only).
- `shared/stalker.ts`: the spider (Kit, Oct 22 playtest: in the game, on by default). `FEEL.creature` is `off | figure | spider | both` (default `both`; rooms run it). One `StalkerState` per night (own seeded rng, in `WoodsState`, stepped in `stepWoods` with every walker, checkpointed with the night), a kit `CreatureMotion` root in creature units at `SPIDER_SIZE` 8.75 m per unit (Froude-scaled). Three modes, no chase, catch or climbing: **Shadow** keeps 80–110 m from everyone, out of beams, or 36–46 m from someone alone with their torch off for 12 s (`Player.isolated`), never a walk that passes near anyone; **Beat** from 4.5–6 min, then about every 4–5 min, a 14 m walk at ~1.1 m/s across someone's view 45–85 m out, in front of a light by the figure's own `planCreature` when there is one, else at the far edge of a level torch beam, starting out of sight (`jump` bumps so clients re-stand its legs); **Flee** when a beam within 38 m and 14° holds on its body or legs for 0.6 s, walking off fast until 70 m from everyone and out of every beam. It never comes within 20 m (`STALKER_MIN`): within 32 m it backs straight off; run down in the dark against an edge, it vanishes to a far spot (never while lit). It steers round trunks it won't pass and gives up a spot it's wedged at for 2.5 s. Events: `spider-drum` (every 25–50 s when someone is 22–60 m off) and `spider-beat`. Snapshots carry `sp` (`stalkerRow`: x, z cm, heading mrad, mode, jump); `Session.spider` is the drawn root (solo between ticks, online from the snapshot buffer). Measured on three 20-min solo nights: 3 beats, 9–12 drums, 0–21 s fleeing, closest 22–31 m. `tests/stalker.test.ts`.
- `src/spider-view.ts`: the kit harvestman legs (`ArachnidLegs`) under that root on each client (presentation; a fixed 60 Hz step between frame poses, velocity eased from the root's travel) drawn with `ArachnidView`; Lab › Spider has the kit's look and gait knobs plus Walk across now and Drum now (solo). Its touchdowns are the footfalls; its eyes (`eyes()`) glint in a torch at 20–40 m whatever the camera look (`Eyeshine.drawSpider`). Sound (`Soundscape.spider`, also in the offline tape render): `spider.step` ticks on the litter from real contacts within ~60 m, `spider.drum` for drums, and the crickets near you hushing from 48 m to silent by 30 m. Both sounds are ElevenLabs takes (`scripts/audio/specs.ts`). `scripts/spider-proof.ts` records a beat across your beam with the offline mix (`--signs`: stills of the web and a bundle). Honest read (Oct 22): at game exposure in your own beam its dark legs barely read at 30–40 m and the eye glint is the tell; against a friend's beam behind it (the Lab's Distant shot) the legs read as silhouettes.
- `shared/spider-signs.ts` + `src/spider-signs-view.ts`: the spider's evidence, reusable (the night's ending can call `placeSpiderSigns(world, seed)` and `webMesh`/`bundleMesh`): a web across the trail between two trunks 2.5–7 m apart (old and irregular, bellied, torn through on one side with the team's pink tape caught in it) and three silk-wrapped bundles 12–40 m off it, hung from 8–12 m up (one the length of a person, lumpy, wound in loose strands). Placed from the night seed; drawn whenever the spider is in the woods.
- `server/`: `room-core.ts` (`WoodsRoomCore`: seats → player ids, bots in empty places, dropped people wait where they stood, per-seat change cursors), `room.ts` (Durable Object), `lobby.ts` (private room codes via `/api/create`), `index.ts` (`gameWorker`, strips `/arcade/barbara`). `tsconfig.server.json` typechecks it with workers types.
- `src/session.ts`: `SoloSession` (60 Hz local sim; `state` is the sim automation writes, `view` draws each friend between its last two ticks via `frame(dt, alpha)`) and `OnlineSession` (`RoomSocket`, `Predictor`/`Smoother` for you, `SnapshotBuffer` + `hermite2` + `Glide` for friends). `main.ts` reads WASD/Shift/E/G/Q, ticks the session and draws `session.view`; `src/camera.ts` (`WoodsCamera`) looks and sways (shared `Walk`) wherever the session puts you. Beam brightness (`beamLevel`) is read on the page's own clock, never the tick or snapshot time.
- Screens: the shared game shell (`@arcade/kit/shell`, [docs/game-shell.md](../../docs/game-shell.md)): the title (BARBARA; name, Play alone, Play with friends, join by code; on a `?room=` page the room's invite, players and Step out of camp) until you first lock the pointer, then the Escape menu (Back to the woods, Settings, Leave) whenever it's free. Settings: volume for master, effects, ambience and voices plus mute (copied into the sound bank), graphics Auto/High/Balanced/Low (`createShellQuality` over `FOREST_QUALITY`, saved as `arcade.quality.barbara.v1`, `?quality=` pins without saving; Auto starts at High and steps down on frame time only, since the renderer's `lastGpuMs` includes CPU encoding and queue waits, which demoted capable GPUs), Subtitles, Camera look (the same five looks as the Lab, saved as `barbara.cameraLook.v1`; `savedCameraLook` ignores it under automation or when the URL sets a camera), reduce camera motion and key labels. Loading waits for the first three cast members (`FriendsView.preload`, 12 s cap) so the searchers are there when Play reveals the camp. M mutes (the shell's). A room is its own page: joining loads `?room=<code>`, Leave room loads solo. The Lab panel uses `capture: "focus"`: it stays open while you play, a click on the woods locks the pointer again, it only takes keys while focused, and while it shows the shell's cards stay hidden (a hint says to click the woods).
- Boundary: the sim's edge wall stands on the state forest's fence line, `boundaryLine` = half − `BOUNDARY_INSET` (3 m), in `shared/world.ts`: `boundarySlabs` run from the line 20 m out past the map's edge (so a walker in the strip is pushed in, never out), `movePlayer` and a checkpoint restore `clampInside` it, and `flareEnv` treats the slabs as walls of any height, so a flare lands inside. `tests/boundary.test.ts`. `src/boundary-view.ts` draws it: a rusted three-strand wire fence on posts with orange-painted tops, orange bands on the line's trunks and a sign every six posts facing in (POSTED / NO TRESPASSING, STATE FOREST / BOUNDARY); paint and signs use `MAT.tape` with a gain so they carry ~15 m in a torch. The held map prints it as a red dashed line with the land past it tinted and labelled STATE FOREST BOUNDARY · POSTED. The first time you come within 12 m a toast says what it is.
- `src/world-view.ts`: the only module that turns world data into pictures (greybox cylinders, cones and boxes today). See "Swapping the look" below.
- `src/tape-view.ts`: flagging tape as drawn (from `world-view.ts`): a 2.5 cm fluorescent-pink band pulled taut over the drawn trunk at 1.4 m (`drawnTrunk` in `@arcade/kit/forest/trunk` mirrors the forest's rings, lobes, lean and per-chunk near/far trunk; the band follows the trunk's sway via `forestTrunkSway`), on a slight diagonal with overlapping ends, a knot a little to one side of whoever tied it, two uneven verlet tails that splay and twist in the forest's gust (`forestWindAt`) colliding with the bark, a bark-fitted translucent contact shadow on the nearest four, and a minimum on-screen width plus the `tapeShine` gain for range. The forest's batch shader lights kit `MAT.tape` glossy and two-sided with back-light through it, and keeps its fluorescent hue under bright light (`tapeFluoresce`). `tests/tape-view.test.ts` checks it never enters the bark and hugs it; `scripts/capture.ts --shots tape1,tape5,tape20` and `scripts/tape-clip.ts` are its stills and wind clip.
- `src/friends-view.ts`: friends on the shared human rig, torch in hand; returns their beams, each direction on a critically damped spring (`beamSettle`) and brightness eased (Lab › Camera › Friends; `?smooth=0` draws them raw for comparisons). Realistic people (`src/people-view.ts` over `@arcade/kit/people`, torch hand on the kit's `torchCarry`) are the default; Lab › Friends › Realistic people or `?people=blocky` draws the blocky ones. A friend with the map up (`mapRaise`, a room sends up/down and the view eases it) faces their view heading, holds a low-detail sheet open at the lower chest in both hands (left hand in a flat sheet grip, head bowed), and their torch drops to the reading level pointing ahead and down.
- `src/camera-look.ts`: camera looks (Eyes = today, Body cam night, Camcorder NightShot, Phone night mode, VHS) and eight realism effects (kit `camera-artifacts.ts` lens/codec passes around the kit `CameraFilter`, one `FinalPassChain` on `renderer.setFinalPass`; all off by default, nothing in the chain at Today). Lab › Camera look (`woods.cameraLook`, `woods.artifacts.*`, `woods.camera.*`, `woods.eyeshine`, `woods.irLight`). It reads `forest.camera.exposure` and never sets exposure or fog. `?lab=camera` (`stageCameraLab`, command `woods.camera.stage`) puts you at the fixed spot 70 m north of camp with two friends' torches; Lab station `woods-camera`. `src/eyeshine.ts`: eyes in your beam, presentation only (a local seeded schedule plus distant-sound events within 75 m). `scripts/camera-lab.ts`: every look and each effect off/on at the spot, plus paired GPU deltas (`--only id,id`), output in `captures/woods-camera`.
- Lake water (kit `water/lake*`, forest `lakeLook`): Today by default; Lab › Water › Water look picks Moonlit (shared-sky reflection cube, canopy mask, lunar glitter path). `?lab=water` (solo) applies the moonlit preset at the dock with a draggable log (Edit/Play) and the panel open. `scripts/lake-proof.ts` (root, mini only) captures Today/Moonlit stills and checks the log input; ledger `perf/lake-water.md`.
- `src/looks.ts`: curated **Looks** (Today, Clear moonlit, New moon pitch black, Ground fog, Storm coming, Downpour), each a bundle of forest look, storm and feel values. `applyLook` sets every key any Look touches, from Today's values captured at start (`lookBase`), so Today is the game as shipped and switching never leaves a setting behind. Picked from Lab › Looks & Triggers › Look (`woods.look`, `?look=<id>`, automation `woods.looks.pick`); the Triggers rows there fire lightning near/mid/far, thunder only, a gust, the creature, a distant sound or a road flare now (`woods.trigger(what)`). `?lab=looks` stages the camera-lab spot with the panel open; Lab station `barbara-looks`. In a room, the Look and every Trigger are this machine's only (presentation, like the Lab's creature trigger): nothing goes over the wire. Scheduled strikes, though, come from the sim clock (`state.t`, the room's in a room) and the world seed, placed round camp (`SharedStormClock`, `sharedPlan` in kit `weather/storm.ts`), so everyone in a room on the same storm Look sees the same strike at the same moment and hears it after their own travel.
- The storm: kit `weather/storm.ts` `StormStrikes` (strike schedule, near/far band, cloud share, `dark` thunder-only strikes, flash limiter and Full/Reduced/Off flashes, `ThunderEvent` arriving slant/343 s after the stroke) and kit `forest/storm.ts` `ForestStorm` (rain easing in, slow soak, gust envelope, the eye's flash-then-darker adaptation over ~3 s while the forest's meter holds). The forest renderer draws the flash as a directional light from the channel through its own shadow map, a dim cloud dome, the far channel in canopy gaps, wet bark/litter/puddles and rain through the weather layer's light-volume mode (drops only in beams and lamp cones; drips under the nearest crowns and cabin eaves). `src/audio/storm.ts` `StormSound` plays thunder through the soundscape's outdoor bus with HRTF, distance low-pass, forest reverb and a synthesized roll, plus the rain beds, drips and gust rush; samples are `STORM_SFX` in `src/sfx.ts` (served by `audioAssets`). `scripts/thunder-proof.ts` renders near/mid/far strikes offline through `WoodsMix` (node-web-audio-api from a scratch dir) and checks onset against travel: 2026-10-22 errors ≤ 12 ms at 404/1201/3001 m. `scripts/looks-proof.ts` takes a still per Look and a near-strike peak at the camera-lab spot plus GPU deltas (Downpour vs Today: +0.6–1.4 ms High, +0.3–0.4 ms Low, mini Metal 1600×900).
- The Lab panel is arranged by `src/knobs.ts` (`LabPanel`'s `arrange` option): sections Looks & Triggers (open), Light & Sky, Weather, Forest, Camp, People, Creature, Map & tape, Sound, Camera, Debug, each with folded sub-headings for what were the subsystems' own groups. - `src/map.ts`: Map style **Today**, the paper map and compass as DOM canvases with a CSS 3D raise. Kept one setting away (Lab › Map › Map style) until Kit picks.
- `src/held-map/`: Map style **Held** (default), the survey sheet and a baseplate compass as first-person props in the world. `index.ts` `HeldMap` is the renderer's overlay pass (`Renderer.setOverlayPass`): drawn after TAA onto the resolved image with its own projection (Held FOV, 2 cm near plane), depth and 4× MSAA, so TAA never jitters or ghosts it; it shades with the frame's own light list (every torch, camp lights, bounce) using the forest's falloff, beam profile and GAIN (`shader.ts`), flattening your own beam's profile on the props while reading so the hot spot stays readable. `print.worker.ts` draws the sheet (`print.ts`: 1 ft contours from `terrainHeight`, drainage streams, marsh, woodland and scrub from real tree density, boulders from real rocks, section lines, collar and title block), the compass atlas (`compass-face.ts`) and fibre normals off the main thread; the print is 4K on High and 2K below unless Lab › Map › Print texture says otherwise, and mips are built on the GPU (`mips.ts`). `pencil.ts` appends the player's knowledge to a persistent canvas (friends' paths in blue pencil, tapes as pink crayon crosses) and only the changed rectangle is uploaded. `props.ts` is the geometry (folded paper with rounded creases, curled corners and the pinch dent, jointed leather gloves, ridged bezel, bevelled plate, slack lanyard, torch). ## Sound

- `src/audio/index.ts` `WoodsAudio`: library cues (`src/sfx.ts`), shared quiet footsteps (#398 levels, unchanged) and the soundscape, mixed in `WoodsMix` (feet at master × effects, woods at master × ambience, mute, one −6 dBFS limiter). Nothing is created or fetched before Begin (`audio.begin()` from the start screen's click; the bank's own any-gesture unlock is off) or a Lab audition. Then it plays while you are playing or have the knobs open; a hidden tab, lost pointer lock or the start screen stops every scheduled one-shot and suspends the context, so nothing plays late. Each file is fetched with `res.ok` checked and caught alone; a failed file is skipped (a one-shot uses another take) and fetched again every 10 s; the beds wait for all five bed files. LabPanel › Sound: wind, leaves, crickets, lake/dock, string-light buzz, room tone, road flares, distant-sound loudness, reverb, audition a distant sound, solo a layer; "how often" is a feel knob (`FEEL.distantRate`, solo only; a room uses 1).
- `src/audio/soundscape.ts`: one Web Audio graph for live and `OfflineAudioContext`. Canopy wind from four points 26 m around you, each following `gustAt` (gust fronts sweep downwind at 7 m/s) and scaled by canopy density there and your `windExposure` (ridge ≈ 10 dB over a hollow); trunk creaks and branch knocks at real nearby trees in gusts; brown-noise room tone; leaves; crickets by `insectLevel` (thin out 25–140 m from camp, hush for 7 s after heavy steps); the lake at the nearest shore point, dock creaks, the string lights' synthesized 120 Hz buzz at the nearest bulb; indoors (cabin/mess hall rects) everything outside goes through a 480 Hz low-pass and the walls bed comes in. Positional one-shots get travel delay (343 m/s), `distanceGain` (−6 dB per 3.2× past 20 m), `distanceCutoff` low-pass, HRTF panning and a procedural forest reverb whose share grows with distance.
- Road flares (`Soundscape.flareStrike`, `Soundscape.flares`, layer `flares`): the cap's scrape (`flare.strike`) where it's struck, the catch (`flare.ignite`) `FLARE_SCRAPE` later, and a positional hiss loop (`flare.burn`) on the nearest four burning, `0.75 / max(1.5 m, d)` × level, distance low-pass and reverb. Offline (`flare-proof.ts`): hiss −36.5 LUFS momentary max at 1 m, −45 at 5 m, −60 at 30 m; strike and catch −29 LUFS.
- `shared/distant.ts`: the distant-event schedule as plain seeded data (`DistantState`, `stepDistant`, `holdDistant`, only + − × ÷ √ and `nextRandom`), so a server can own it and every player hears the same sound, take, place and emission time. First after 70–120 s; interval 160 s → 75 s as dread rises (25 min out, 180 m from camp), places 35% closer; never two at once, no repeats of the last three. `stepWoods` holds it while anyone's beam is up as a signal and for `DISTANT_AFTER_SIGNAL` (6 s) after; on the client a distant sound that would reach you during a signal (it was in flight when the signal began) fades out, while one already sounding plays on. `WoodsAudio.event` queues events and plays them in `frame`, after the session has ticked, so the travel already elapsed comes from the same clock.
- The uncanny tier (`UNCANNY_SOUNDS`, Kit: "a howl that becomes… more than a howl", "an animal getting got"): a howl that goes past any breath and becomes a woman's scream, splits into two voices drifting out of tune, or just won't end, each stopping dead (`far.howl`, 170–300 m, may be past the edge); a distress cry, a thrash, a thud and a wet crack, then nothing (`far.taken`, where the creature last crossed if lately and in earshot, and it pulls the creature's next sighting to 50–110 s later); a searcher's "Barbara!" answered by the same take from the far side of the person nearest the caller, flat, the gap between calls drawn out, the last vowel held twice too long (`far.mimic`, `answerCall` from `stepWoods` after the voices, only for `MIMIC_LINES`; it holds the other sounds until it answers and is dropped 10 s late); the loon twice sample-identical (`far.echo`); a big trunk creaking in steps under a climbing weight (`far.climb`); every cricket stopping at once, then one knock (`far.stop`, only with someone near camp or the shore). Its own seeded stream (`DistantState.odd`); none before `UNCANNY_FIRST` (8 min), then a due sound is uncanny with `uncannyChance(dread)` (0 under dread 0.3, 0.7 by 0.75), each at most once a night. `HUSH` in `soundscape.ts` sets how the crickets react (the howl's dead stop: 16 s of silence), `REVERB` the forest's share. Fetched on the soundscape's second load pass (10 s after Begin; offline renders and Lab triggers load everything). Lab › Sound › Uncanny: on, how likely (`FEEL.uncanny`, `uncannyRate`; a room uses the defaults), Trigger for each (solo in the sim; the mimic stages a call 70–110 m ahead; a room: this machine only, no mimic).
- Sources: ElevenLabs `eleven_text_to_sound_v2` (Kit's account, commercial licence), 82 + 57 takes, hand-picked from contact sheets, Scribe audio-event tags and pitch tracks; `scripts/audio/specs.ts` holds prompts, cuts and picks, `scripts/audio/sounds.ts generate|curate|tag|build` reproduces `public/audio/*.ogg` and `src/audio/files.ts` (per-file provenance, request id, loudness; ids with nothing in the work directory keep their shipped files). The uncanny tier's 55 ingredient takes are `UNCANNY_SOURCE_SPECS`; `scripts/audio/uncanny.ts analyze|compose|check` (DSP in `dsp.ts`: YIN pitch, WSOLA stretch, bends that follow the howl's own note, 2 ms dead stops; `check` asks Scribe what each file sounds like) writes `$WORK/composed/`, which `build` encodes. 67 files, 1.98 MB Opus (the uncanny 21 are 0.76 MB). Credits in `public/CREDITS.md`. Listening page: gallery `barbara-sounds-2026-10-22/`.
- Proof: `scripts/audio-proof.ts` (headless, muted, HiveMind browser) walks camp → woods on a 30 fps synthetic clock, records `WoodsAudio.tape`, renders it offline through `WoodsMix` and muxes `walk.mp4`, then renders every layer and distant sound soloed with EBU R128 levels. ## Swapping the look

A real forest renderer replaces `src/world-view.ts` internals and keeps its entry points; the sim never changes.

- `WorldView.setWorld(world)`: upload static content. Called again when the world regenerates (trunk density knob).
- `WorldView.draw(fp, world, state, t, eye, feel)`: per-frame content (tapes from `state.tapes`, the ridge beacon, the sky dome).
- `WorldView.lights(world, t, feel)`: the place's `SceneLight`s and `FogLamp`s (mess hall windows, string lights, porches, ridge beacon).
- `WorldView.occluders(world)`: trunk/rock/building footprints for polar shadows.
- `shared/forest-world.ts` `toForestWorld(world, tapes)` / `forestFlags(world, tapes)`: the same data in the look agent's `ForestWorld` shape (branch `woods-look`, `@arcade/kit/sim/forest-world`). Its types are a structural mirror until that file merges; then import them. Swapping to `createForestView` means feeding it `toForestWorld(world)`, flags from `forestFlags` each frame, and torches from the beams `main.ts` already builds.
- Data to read: `world.trees` (`id, x, z, y, species: pine|fir|birch|snag, radius, height, seed`), `world.rocks` (`x, z, y, w, d, h, yaw, seed`), `world.buildings` (`kind, rect, y, h, windows`), `world.lamps`, `world.lake` (`x, z, rx, rz, depth, level`), `world.trail.points`, `world.markers`, `world.ridgeLight`, `world.camp`, and `terrainHeight(world.terrain, x, z)` as the one ground height. Tapes are `{ id, tree, x, y, z, yaw, by, at }` (yaw = plan angle of the knot from the trunk centre, `atan2(dz, dx)`).
