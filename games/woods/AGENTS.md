# Woods

- Unlisted co-op toy at `/arcade/woods/`: solo with bots, or a room of 2–6 shared by link (`?room=<code>`). Keep `noindex`, no landing panel. Kit: "in the woods… we're exploring something together." Concepts 1 (flashlights on a forest trail), 2 (fog survey with compass, map and flagging tape) and 4 (summer camp '87, the spawn and base) are one game.
- Phase 2 (rooms) is built on `@arcade/kit/server` (Archers' pattern: kit `RoomCore` + `RoomObject` + `LobbyObject`). Kit holds deploys of woods until he says; keep it unlisted.

## Layout

- `shared/world.ts`: the forest as plain data from `WorldParams` (seed, half size, trunk density, marker count). Terrain height (`terrainHeight`/`groundHeight`, `standHeight` adds the dock), lake, camp buildings and place lights, the old path, trees, rocks, survey markers, the ridge mast light, a tree grid for reach and collisions (`treesNear`, `solidsNear`). No trig or `Math.random` in generation, so a server builds the same world.
- `shared/sim.ts`: `WoodsState` (players, bot brains, tapes, found markers, map knowledge per player, `rev` counters, the sound cursor) and `stepWoods(state, world, inputs, dt, feel)`, authoritative in the room and run locally solo. `stepMotion` (look, map raise, `movePlayer`) is the one walking step shared by the room, solo and client prediction. `PlayerInput` is `{ yaw, pitch, light, tie, map, mx, mz, sprint }`. Ids: solo you are 0, room people 1–6, bots from `BOT_ID` (100); bots follow the nearest person and answer any signaller. `FEEL` is every feel constant; its knobs are `src/feel-controls.ts` (kept out of `shared/` so the server has no DOM types).
- `shared/protocol.ts`: wire format (JSON). 30 Hz room, 15 Hz snapshots; inputs quantized (`packIntent`) before the client predicts with them. Snapshots carry compact player rows, your authoritative motion and ack (`you`, `ack`), and tapes, finds, names and your map's new pencil marks only when they change. `TEAM` 4 (bots fill up to it), `GRACE_MS` 90 s seat hold, `ROOM_FEEL` = `FEEL` (Lab walking knobs don't change the room).
- `shared/sounds.ts`: **hook for the audio layer.** A seeded distant-sound schedule (`soundAt(seed, k, world)`, `soundsBetween(seed, from, to, world)`; kinds `owl|loon|branch-crack|coyote|knock|camp-bell|radio-static` with a world position and gain, ~32 s apart). `stepWoods` emits `{ kind: "sound", sound }` events as the room clock passes each, so every player hears it at the same time and place; `state.sounds.seed` comes in the join snapshot for scheduling ahead. Nothing plays them yet.
- `server/`: `room-core.ts` (`WoodsRoomCore`: seats → player ids, bots in empty places, dropped people wait where they stood, per-seat change cursors), `room.ts` (Durable Object), `lobby.ts` (private room codes via `/api/create`), `index.ts` (`gameWorker`, strips `/arcade/woods`). `tsconfig.server.json` typechecks it with workers types.
- `src/session.ts`: `SoloSession` (60 Hz local sim) and `OnlineSession` (`RoomSocket`, `Predictor`/`Smoother` for you, `SnapshotBuffer` + `hermite2` + `Glide` for friends). `main.ts` reads WASD/Shift/E/G, ticks the session and draws `session.view`; `src/camera.ts` (`WoodsCamera`) looks and sways (shared `Walk`) wherever the session puts you.
- `src/world-view.ts`: the only module that turns world data into pictures (greybox cylinders, cones and boxes today). See "Swapping the look" below.
- `src/friends-view.ts`: friends on the shared human rig, torch in hand; returns their beams.
- `src/map.ts`: the paper map and compass (DOM canvases, CSS 3D raise), printed from the shared terrain function; pencil layer from `MapKnowledge`. Lit by your flashlight or camp light.
- `src/shaders.ts`: stock physical lights + shared fog medium, plus two tags (sky dome, tape). `src/audio.ts`: library placeholders + shared quiet footsteps.
- Tests: `tests/room.test.ts` (in-process room: joins, bots, reconciliation, shared tapes, map swap, finds, rejoin, bandwidth). `scripts/ws-smoke.ts` (two raw sockets against a running Worker). `scripts/mp-test.ts` (three headless muted browsers: join by link, beams, a shared tape, map swap, drop and rejoin, KB/s; `--serve <port>` runs its own `wrangler dev` after a build, which HiveMind workers need since they are off the tailnet).
- Tailnet preview with rooms: `bun run dev:server` (build + `wrangler dev` on 127.0.0.1:5298) behind `tailscale serve --https=5298`; `https://studio.taila8e7c7.ts.net:5298/arcade/woods/`. Tailnet-only.
- `scripts/capture.ts` (stills at scripted poses: camp, hall, beam, signal, ridge, marker, trail, map; `--set key=value` overrides settings) and `scripts/clip.ts` (tie + map clip). Both headless and muted; run through `hivemind run --kind browser` after a build, output in `captures/woods`.

## Swapping the look

A real forest renderer replaces `src/world-view.ts` internals and keeps its entry points; the sim never changes.

- `WorldView.setWorld(world)`: upload static content. Called again when the world regenerates (trunk density knob).
- `WorldView.draw(fp, world, state, t, eye, feel)`: per-frame content (tapes from `state.tapes`, the ridge beacon, the sky dome).
- `WorldView.lights(world, t, feel)`: the place's `SceneLight`s and `FogLamp`s (mess hall windows, string lights, porches, ridge beacon).
- `WorldView.occluders(world)`: trunk/rock/building footprints for polar shadows.
- `shared/forest-world.ts` `toForestWorld(world, tapes)` / `forestFlags(world, tapes)`: the same data in the look agent's `ForestWorld` shape (branch `woods-look`, `@arcade/kit/sim/forest-world`). Its types are a structural mirror until that file merges; then import them. Swapping to `createForestView` means feeding it `toForestWorld(world)`, flags from `forestFlags` each frame, and torches from the beams `main.ts` already builds.
- Data to read: `world.trees` (`id, x, z, y, species: pine|fir|birch|snag, radius, height, seed`), `world.rocks` (`x, z, y, w, d, h, yaw, seed`), `world.buildings` (`kind, rect, y, h, windows`), `world.lamps`, `world.lake` (`x, z, rx, rz, depth, level`), `world.trail.points`, `world.markers`, `world.ridgeLight`, `world.camp`, and `terrainHeight(world.terrain, x, z)` as the one ground height. Tapes are `{ id, tree, x, y, z, yaw, by, at }` (yaw = plan angle of the knot from the trunk centre, `atan2(dz, dx)`).
