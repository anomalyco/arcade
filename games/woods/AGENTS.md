# Woods

- Unlisted co-op toy at `/arcade/woods/`: solo with bots, or a room of 2–6 shared by link (`?room=<code>`). Keep `noindex`, no landing panel. Kit: "in the woods… we're exploring something together." Concepts 1 (flashlights on a forest trail), 2 (fog survey with compass, map and flagging tape) and 4 (summer camp '87, the spawn and base) are one game.
- Phase 2 (rooms) is built on `@arcade/kit/server` (Archers' pattern: kit `RoomCore` + `RoomObject` + `LobbyObject`). Kit holds deploys of woods until he says; keep it unlisted.

## Layout

- `shared/world.ts`: the forest as plain data from `WorldParams` (seed, half size, trunk density, marker count). Terrain height (`terrainHeight`/`groundHeight`, `standHeight` adds the dock), lake, camp buildings and place lights, the old path, trees, rocks, survey markers, the ridge mast light, a tree grid for reach and collisions (`treesNear`, `solidsNear`). No trig or `Math.random` in generation, so a server builds the same world.
- `shared/sim.ts`: `WoodsState` (players, bot brains, tapes, found markers, map knowledge per player, `rev` counters, the distant-sound schedule) and `stepWoods(state, world, inputs, dt, feel)`, authoritative in the room and run locally solo. `stepMotion` (look, map raise, `movePlayer`) is the one walking step shared by the room, solo and client prediction. `PlayerInput` is `{ yaw, pitch, light, tie, map, mx, mz, sprint }`. Ids: solo you are 0, room people 1–6, bots from `BOT_ID` (100); bots follow the nearest person and answer any signaller. `FEEL` is every feel constant; its knobs are `src/feel-controls.ts` (kept out of `shared/` so the server has no DOM types).
- `shared/protocol.ts`: wire format (JSON). 30 Hz room, 15 Hz snapshots; inputs quantized (`packIntent`) before the client predicts with them. Snapshots carry compact player rows, your authoritative motion and ack (`you`, `ack`), finds and names when they change, and your map as a `MapDelta` (new path points, tape ids learned/gone, copied paths' appended points; `applyMapDelta` on the client). The tape list comes once on join; `tape-tied`/`tape-removed` events keep it after. Everything a room holds is bounded: bots keep `botTapes` up each and only tie bare trunks, `MAX_TAPES` in all, removed tapes leave every map, copies of a leaver's path are dropped, and paths are append-only with spacing that widens every 500 samples (`TRAIL_MAX` 2000). `scripts/bandwidth-hour.ts` measures a simulated hour (6 people: ~4.0 KB/s per seat, flat). `TEAM` 4 (bots fill up to it), `GRACE_MS` 90 s seat hold, `ROOM_FEEL` = `FEEL` (Lab walking knobs don't change the room).
- `shared/distant.ts`: the distant-sound schedule (see Sound); `shared/voices.ts`: the searchers' voices (see Story and voices). `stepWoods` steps it at the tick rate with the people (not bots) and pushes `DistantEvent`s (`kind: "distant"`) into `state.events`; rooms broadcast them like any event and send `DistantState` once on join (`Snapshot.distant`). Clients only play events.
- `server/`: `room-core.ts` (`WoodsRoomCore`: seats → player ids, bots in empty places, dropped people wait where they stood, per-seat change cursors), `room.ts` (Durable Object), `lobby.ts` (private room codes via `/api/create`), `index.ts` (`gameWorker`, strips `/arcade/woods`). `tsconfig.server.json` typechecks it with workers types.
- `src/session.ts`: `SoloSession` (60 Hz local sim; `state` is the sim automation writes, `view` draws each friend between its last two ticks via `frame(dt, alpha)`) and `OnlineSession` (`RoomSocket`, `Predictor`/`Smoother` for you, `SnapshotBuffer` + `hermite2` + `Glide` for friends). `main.ts` reads WASD/Shift/E/G, ticks the session and draws `session.view`; `src/camera.ts` (`WoodsCamera`) looks and sways (shared `Walk`) wherever the session puts you. Beam brightness (`beamLevel`) is read on the page's own clock, never the tick or snapshot time.
- Screens: the title (Play alone / Play with friends, or the room's invite) until you first lock the pointer, then the Escape menu whenever it's free (`index.html`). Both carry `src/menu.ts` `SettingsMenu` (master/effects/ambience volume and mute on the sound bank's saved volumes, graphics Auto/High/Balanced/Low). The quality pick is saved as `woods.quality`; `?quality=` overrides without saving. Auto starts at High and steps down on frame time only (the renderer's `lastGpuMs` includes CPU encoding and queue waits, which demoted capable GPUs). The Lab panel uses `capture: "focus"`: it stays open while you play, a click on the woods locks the pointer again, and it only takes keys while focused.
- `src/world-view.ts`: the only module that turns world data into pictures (greybox cylinders, cones and boxes today). See "Swapping the look" below.
- `src/tape-view.ts`: flagging tape as drawn (from `world-view.ts`): a 2.5 cm fluorescent-pink band pulled taut over the drawn trunk at 1.4 m (`drawnTrunk` in `@arcade/kit/forest/trunk` mirrors the forest's rings, lobes, lean and per-chunk near/far trunk; the band follows the trunk's sway via `forestTrunkSway`), on a slight diagonal with overlapping ends, a knot a little to one side of whoever tied it, two uneven verlet tails that splay and twist in the forest's gust (`forestWindAt`) colliding with the bark, a bark-fitted translucent contact shadow on the nearest four, and a minimum on-screen width plus the `tapeShine` gain for range. The forest's batch shader lights kit `MAT.tape` glossy and two-sided with back-light through it, and keeps its fluorescent hue under bright light (`tapeFluoresce`). `tests/tape-view.test.ts` checks it never enters the bark and hugs it; `scripts/capture.ts --shots tape1,tape5,tape20` and `scripts/tape-clip.ts` are its stills and wind clip.
- `src/friends-view.ts`: friends on the shared human rig, torch in hand; returns their beams, each direction on a critically damped spring (`beamSettle`) and brightness eased (Lab › Camera › Friends; `?smooth=0` draws them raw for comparisons). Realistic people stay opt-in (`?people=real`) until the people rework lands.
- `src/camera-look.ts`: camera looks (Eyes = today, Body cam night, Camcorder NightShot, Phone night mode, VHS) and eight realism effects (kit `camera-artifacts.ts` lens/codec passes around the kit `CameraFilter`, one `FinalPassChain` on `renderer.setFinalPass`; all off by default, nothing in the chain at Today). Lab › Camera look (`woods.cameraLook`, `woods.artifacts.*`, `woods.camera.*`, `woods.eyeshine`, `woods.irLight`). It reads `forest.camera.exposure` and never sets exposure or fog. `?lab=camera` (`stageCameraLab`, command `woods.camera.stage`) puts you at the fixed spot 70 m north of camp with two friends' torches; Lab station `woods-camera`. `src/eyeshine.ts`: eyes in your beam, presentation only (a local seeded schedule plus distant-sound events within 75 m). `scripts/camera-lab.ts`: every look and each effect off/on at the spot, plus paired GPU deltas (`--only id,id`), output in `captures/woods-camera`.
- `src/map.ts`: the paper map and compass (DOM canvases, CSS 3D raise), printed from the shared terrain function; pencil layer from `MapKnowledge`. Lit by your flashlight or camp light.
- `src/shaders.ts`: stock physical lights + shared fog medium, plus two tags (sky dome, tape). `src/audio/`: the soundscape (see Sound below), library cues and the shared quiet footsteps.
- Tests: `tests/room.test.ts` (in-process room: joins, bots, reconciliation, shared tapes, map swap, finds, rejoin, bandwidth). `scripts/ws-smoke.ts` (two raw sockets against a running Worker). `scripts/mp-test.ts` (three headless muted browsers: join by link, beams, a shared tape, map swap, drop and rejoin, KB/s; `--serve <port>` runs its own `wrangler dev` after a build, which HiveMind workers need since they are off the tailnet).
- Tailnet preview with rooms: `bun run dev:server` (build + `wrangler dev` on 127.0.0.1:5298) behind `tailscale serve --https=5298`; `https://studio.taila8e7c7.ts.net:5298/arcade/woods/`. Tailnet-only.
- `scripts/jitter.ts` (friends' drawn position and beam jitter, raw against smoothed: solo on synthetic 120 Hz and wobbling 60 Hz clocks, and a two-browser room) and `scripts/smooth-proof.ts` (raw/smoothed side-by-side clip of a camper crossing camp, title, Escape menu and Lab-panel stills). `--serve <port>` / static `dist` like the others; HiveMind fetches only the repo-root `captures/`, so copy `games/woods/captures/` back from the worker.
- `scripts/capture.ts` (stills at scripted poses: camp, hall, beam, signal, ridge, marker, trail, map; `--set key=value` overrides settings) and `scripts/clip.ts` (tie + map clip). Both headless and muted; run through `hivemind run --kind browser` after a build, output in `captures/woods`.

## Sound

- `src/audio/index.ts` `WoodsAudio`: library cues (`src/sfx.ts`), shared quiet footsteps (#398 levels, unchanged) and the soundscape, mixed in `WoodsMix` (feet at master × effects, woods at master × ambience, mute, one −6 dBFS limiter). Nothing is created or fetched before Begin (`audio.begin()` from the start screen's click; the bank's own any-gesture unlock is off) or a Lab audition. Then it plays while you are playing or have the knobs open; a hidden tab, lost pointer lock or the start screen stops every scheduled one-shot and suspends the context, so nothing plays late. Each file is fetched with `res.ok` checked and caught alone; a failed file is skipped (a one-shot uses another take) and fetched again every 10 s; the beds wait for all five bed files. LabPanel › Sound: wind, leaves, crickets, lake/dock, string-light buzz, room tone, distant-sound loudness, reverb, audition a distant sound, solo a layer; "how often" is a feel knob (`FEEL.distantRate`, solo only; a room uses 1).
- `src/audio/soundscape.ts`: one Web Audio graph for live and `OfflineAudioContext`. Canopy wind from four points 26 m around you, each following `gustAt` (gust fronts sweep downwind at 7 m/s) and scaled by canopy density there and your `windExposure` (ridge ≈ 10 dB over a hollow); trunk creaks and branch knocks at real nearby trees in gusts; brown-noise room tone; leaves; crickets by `insectLevel` (thin out 25–140 m from camp, hush for 7 s after heavy steps); the lake at the nearest shore point, dock creaks, the string lights' synthesized 120 Hz buzz at the nearest bulb; indoors (cabin/mess hall rects) everything outside goes through a 480 Hz low-pass and the walls bed comes in. Positional one-shots get travel delay (343 m/s), `distanceGain` (−6 dB per 3.2× past 20 m), `distanceCutoff` low-pass, HRTF panning and a procedural forest reverb whose share grows with distance.
- `shared/distant.ts`: the distant-event schedule as plain seeded data (`DistantState`, `stepDistant`, `holdDistant`, only + − × ÷ √ and `nextRandom`), so a server can own it and every player hears the same sound, take, place and emission time. First after 70–120 s; interval 160 s → 75 s as dread rises (25 min out, 180 m from camp), places 35% closer; never two at once, no repeats of the last three. `stepWoods` holds it while anyone's beam is up as a signal and for `DISTANT_AFTER_SIGNAL` (6 s) after; on the client a distant sound that would reach you during a signal (it was in flight when the signal began) fades out, while one already sounding plays on. `WoodsAudio.event` queues events and plays them in `frame`, after the session has ticked, so the travel already elapsed comes from the same clock.
- Sources: ElevenLabs `eleven_text_to_sound_v2` (Kit's account, commercial licence), 82 + 57 takes, hand-picked from contact sheets, Scribe audio-event tags and pitch tracks; `scripts/audio/specs.ts` holds prompts, cuts and picks, `scripts/audio/sounds.ts generate|curate|tag|build` reproduces `public/audio/*.ogg` and `src/audio/files.ts` (per-file provenance, request id, loudness). 46 files, 1.26 MB Opus. Credits in `public/CREDITS.md`.
- Proof: `scripts/audio-proof.ts` (headless, muted, HiveMind browser) walks camp → woods on a 30 fps synthetic clock, records `WoodsAudio.tape`, renders it offline through `WoodsMix` and muxes `walk.mp4`, then renders every layer and distant sound soloed with EBU R128 levels. Measured (Chrome, master 80%, 2026-10-21): walk −39.7 LUFS integrated, −15 dBFS peak; your footsteps' loudest 100 ms sit 9 dB under the bed at camp and 4 dB under in the woods (#398 measured 5–13); ridge −40.6 vs hollow −52.1 LUFS; distant sounds −32.5…−37.7 LUFS momentary max at 55–520 m. Listening page: gallery `woods-2026-10-21/audio/`. For mix iteration without a browser job, the same `WoodsMix` renders under Bun with `node-web-audio-api` (its HRTF and convolver come out about 4 dB quieter than Chrome's; decode the Opus files to WAV in `Soundscape.load`'s fetcher).

## Story and voices

- `STORY.md` is act 1 of Find Barbara (Kit's pick over D-58): November 1996, Barbara K. Nakamura's Lexus in the camp lot, the players volunteers on the night search. It holds the cast, the opening and the key-moment options for Kit.
- `shared/voices.ts`: the voice director, plain seeded data (`VoiceState`, `stepVoices`, only + − × ÷ √ and `nextRandom`) stepped in `stepWoods` after the distant sounds, so a room and every client hear the same line from the same place at the same emission time (`VoiceEvent`, `kind: "voice"`, broadcast like any event; no join state needed). `voiceRoles`: people take Owen, Tess, Dale, Gary, Walt, Joyce; bots by id take `BOT_ORDER` (Walt, Joyce, Gary, Dale, Tess) and are named after them; anyone left is a roamer wandering 50–140 m out. Chatter every (30–70 s) / `FEEL.chatter` (calls, check-ins, where-are-you); reactions with cooldowns to finds, the creature (read by kind string), signals, distant sounds, fresh tape, a torch starting to die, a person alone over 25 s. One voice at a time (`busyUntil` covers travel to the furthest person), nothing over a distant sound, the distant sounds wait for the voices (`holdDistant`). Only lines loud enough to reach a person are said (`CARRY`: shout 400 m, call 160, talk 30, quiet 12). People's searchers speak only key lines (find, sighting) and not with `FEEL.voicePeople` 0; chatter 0 leaves only key lines. Nothing but key lines for the first `OPENING_SECONDS` (30 s).
- The opening is per client (`openingLines`, `src/story.ts` `StoryHud`): at your first step out, Walt's line then five calls for Barbara at 9–150 m around and ahead (two on later nights, `woods.nights`), and the FIND BARBARA card at 5 s (1.5 s later nights), fading on its own. Subtitles show a line as it reaches you if it's over −54 LUFS there (setting `woods.subtitles`, the menu's Subtitles button).
- `src/audio/voices.ts` `VoiceBus` (in `WoodsMix`, master × the kit's `volumeVoice`, the menu's Voices slider): travel delay, `voiceGain` (−6 dB per 3.2× past 5 m), air low-pass plus trunks between speaker and you (`trunksBetween`: each takes ~0.3 dB and pulls the low-pass down), HRTF, its own forest reverb, one dull echo off the hills on shouts past 50 m, the walls' low-pass indoors. LabPanel › Voices: volume, subtitles, falloff, trunk muffling, reverb; Feel › Voices: chatter, player barks.
- Lines: `scripts/audio/voice-specs.ts` (cast, voice ids, every directed line), `scripts/audio/voices.ts generate|measure|stt|failed|build` (Eleven v4, premade voices, stability 0.45; measure checks shouts against the same voice's talk: pitch 1.9–3.1× and brighter; Scribe catches spoken directions and v4's habit of saying a short line two or three times, which is cut after the first reading at the line's last word). Builds `public/audio/voice/*.ogg` (141 lines, 2.2 MB, momentary max by class: shout −14, call −18, talk −26, quiet −32 LUFS), `shared/lines.gen.ts`, `src/audio/voice-files.ts`. Work files in `$TMPDIR/opencode/woods-voice`.
- Proof: `scripts/opening-proof.ts` (headless, muted, HiveMind browser): the opening on a synthetic clock with card and subtitles in frame, rendered offline through `WoodsMix`, each line's distance, trunks and level where it arrived (`opening.json`), a voices-only waveform.

## Swapping the look

A real forest renderer replaces `src/world-view.ts` internals and keeps its entry points; the sim never changes.

- `WorldView.setWorld(world)`: upload static content. Called again when the world regenerates (trunk density knob).
- `WorldView.draw(fp, world, state, t, eye, feel)`: per-frame content (tapes from `state.tapes`, the ridge beacon, the sky dome).
- `WorldView.lights(world, t, feel)`: the place's `SceneLight`s and `FogLamp`s (mess hall windows, string lights, porches, ridge beacon).
- `WorldView.occluders(world)`: trunk/rock/building footprints for polar shadows.
- `shared/forest-world.ts` `toForestWorld(world, tapes)` / `forestFlags(world, tapes)`: the same data in the look agent's `ForestWorld` shape (branch `woods-look`, `@arcade/kit/sim/forest-world`). Its types are a structural mirror until that file merges; then import them. Swapping to `createForestView` means feeding it `toForestWorld(world)`, flags from `forestFlags` each frame, and torches from the beams `main.ts` already builds.
- Data to read: `world.trees` (`id, x, z, y, species: pine|fir|birch|snag, radius, height, seed`), `world.rocks` (`x, z, y, w, d, h, yaw, seed`), `world.buildings` (`kind, rect, y, h, windows`), `world.lamps`, `world.lake` (`x, z, rx, rz, depth, level`), `world.trail.points`, `world.markers`, `world.ridgeLight`, `world.camp`, and `terrainHeight(world.terrain, x, z)` as the one ground height. Tapes are `{ id, tree, x, y, z, yaw, by, at }` (yaw = plan angle of the knot from the trunk centre, `atan2(dz, dx)`).
