# Night Shift ’87

- Unlisted first-person cubicle office at `/arcade/nightshift/`, rendered with `@arcade/kit`. Keep `noindex` and omit a landing panel.
- `src/world.ts` owns metre-scale procedural geometry, colliders and the wide/phone comparison cameras. Eye height is 1.65 m and vertical FOV is 68°.
- `src/shaders.ts` composes physical lighting, spot-map shadows, analytic blind gobos, procedural surface recipes and shared fog. Shared screen-space contact shadows run before fog (Shadows panel; omitted under `?smoke`, as in Lot). Derivatives belong at fragment entry, before divergent material branches.
- `src/settings.ts` exposes lighting, air, movement, audio and handset travel through the shared Backquote panel.
- Loading: `@arcade/kit/client/boot-screen` paints a phosphor boot log from index.html (`scripts/boot-screen.ts` plugin) before the 1.7 MB bundle arrives. `main.ts` starts every file in `src/assets.ts` at once with streamed byte progress (the carpet decodes alongside the props), one line per stage, and the Begin prompt (inside the boot screen) waits for the first frame's GPU work, and clicks and pointer lock are ignored until then; Begin fades the screen to black into the opening. Bar weights in `BOOT_PLAN` are measured milliseconds. `scripts/load-timing.ts` measures cold, muted loads to Begin (`--compare <saved dist>` A/B, `--mbps` throttle, `--profile`; use `--worker studio`, other workers are too noisy); `scripts/boot-proof.ts` takes the still and handoff clip, and `--fail` checks DISK ERROR and Retry.
- `src/opening.ts` owns the seated opening, token drain, phone whip, floor reveal and first-movement standing. The scene starts black behind Begin. Input resumes at the settled pose; focus loss and the panel pause the timeline. Space skips only after a completed first view. Reduce camera motion removes whip overshoot and blur. `terminal.ts` binds an animated canvas texture to the authored CRT and red zero-token screens to colleagues' CRTs.
- `shared/call.ts` owns ringing → pickup → call → hangup → ended. `src/audio.ts` synthesizes the descending tone/bells/hum and plays `public/audio/kit-call.mp3` against `shared/kit-call.json` cues. Kit authorized his ElevenLabs v4 voice for the section-1 caller. `scripts/generate-beat1.ts` and `process-beat1.py` reproduce ten stems and the telephone treatment without playback. Decode readiness gates answering. The first playthrough forces the full call through click/dial tone and automatic hangup; if the opening was already seen at page load, E can hang up after the first line. `src/elevator.ts` owns the far-wall doorway, closing leaves and final fade; there is no basement encounter yet. Earlier call scripts remain historical.
- Physical WASD/arrows move, mouse looks after a click, E answers/hangs up, F toggles the torch, M mutes. Audio starts only after a canvas click and is muted while unfocused.
- `bun run check` runs TypeScript, local import-cycle detection, focused tests and a production build. `bun run check:shaders` validates all seven generated shaders with Naga.
- `scripts/capture.ts --input` runs headless and muted through HiveMind, captures the two cameras, and checks pickup/hangup, physical Dvorak movement, flashlight and panel ownership. Use `--fetch captures/nightshift` to retrieve its output.
- `scripts/compare.py` produces a two-view concept/capture sheet, 4× difference images, RGB MAE, linear luminance, hue, histograms and an 8×6 grid. The concepts and scene are not pixel-registered; metrics describe differences rather than perceptual similarity.
- `src/props.ts` loads generated period props and credited CC0/CC-BY models through shared glTF/physical-light/shadow passes. `public/CREDITS.md` records sources, licences and adaptations. `scripts/prepare-generated.ts RAW_DIR OUTPUT_DIR [id]` keeps source geometry, converts the maps to 1K WebP and separates the CRT phosphor glass; preserve raw files outside the repository. `scripts/preview-props.py` renders three neutral angles headlessly. Partition rails must sit proud of fabric and follow the long axis; coplanar trim caused the moving patches. Noise octaves and bump normals fade by pixel footprint.
- The receiver's support span and rest position must fit the actual base. `tests/cradle.test.ts` checks both supports against the shipped GLB. The fitted roll also drives the cord endpoint. Keep one moving receiver.

Export into a separate artifact directory, inspect the three-angle review, then copy the GLBs and `authored.json` into `public/props/authored/`. Keep the editable blends in the gallery. Use `--python-exit-code 1` so Blender script failures fail the job.
- `scripts/answer-video.ts` uses deterministic GPU readback, then composites the actual subtitle text and muxes the saved call without playback. Avoid screenshot/stability waits against its frozen RAF clock. Original captures and generated paint-over targets remain distinct in comparisons (`compare.py --paintover`).
- `scripts/beat1-proof.ts` captures the current opening/call/elevator at 30 fps and four concept-match views. It uses headless Chromium (the headless shell does not acquire pointer lock here), synchronous `Renderer.drawTo`, real physical movement/E input and scripted look directions; the full mix is rendered in OfflineAudioContext and muxed from files. Run on a HiveMind browser worker. `--stills` checks compositions without encoding the tape. `tests/opening.test.ts` covers frame-rate bounds, pause, skip continuity, cue coverage and close-before-fade.
