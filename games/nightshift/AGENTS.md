# Night Shift ’87

- Unlisted first-person cubicle office at `/arcade/nightshift/`, rendered with `@arcade/kit`. Keep `noindex` and omit a landing panel.
- `src/world.ts` owns metre-scale procedural geometry, colliders and the wide/phone comparison cameras. Eye height is 1.65 m and vertical FOV is 68°.
- `src/shaders.ts` composes physical lighting, spot-map shadows, analytic blind gobos, procedural surface recipes and shared fog. Shared screen-space contact shadows run before fog (Shadows panel; omitted under `?smoke`, as in Lot). Derivatives belong at fragment entry, before divergent material branches.
- `src/settings.ts` exposes lighting, air, movement, audio and handset travel through the shared Backquote panel.
- Loading: `@arcade/kit/client/boot-screen` paints a phosphor boot log from index.html (`scripts/boot-screen.ts` plugin) before the 1.7 MB bundle arrives. `main.ts` starts every file in `src/assets.ts` at once with streamed byte progress (the carpet decodes alongside the props), one line per stage, and the Begin prompt (inside the boot screen) waits for the first frame's GPU work, and clicks and pointer lock are ignored until then; Begin fades the screen to black into the opening. Bar weights in `BOOT_PLAN` are measured milliseconds. `scripts/load-timing.ts` measures cold, muted loads to Begin (`--compare <saved dist>` A/B, `--mbps` throttle, `--profile`; use `--worker studio`, other workers are too noisy); `scripts/boot-proof.ts` takes the still and handoff clip, and `--fail` checks DISK ERROR and Retry.
- `src/opening.ts` owns the seated opening, token drain, phone whip, floor reveal and first-movement standing. The scene starts black behind Begin. Input resumes at the settled pose, 8 s after Begin (was 15.5 s; playtest Oct 21, "I want to explore"); focus loss and the panel pause the timeline. Space skips only after a completed first view. Reduce camera motion removes whip overshoot and blur. `terminal.ts` binds an animated canvas texture to the authored CRT and red zero-token screens to colleagues' CRTs.
- `shared/call.ts` owns ringing → pickup → call → hangup → ended. `src/audio.ts` synthesizes the descending tone/bells/hum and plays `public/audio/kit-call.mp3` against `shared/kit-call.json` cues. Kit authorized his ElevenLabs v4 voice for the section-1 caller. `scripts/generate-beat1.ts` and `process-beat1.py` reproduce ten stems and the telephone treatment without playback. Decode readiness gates answering. Walking remains available during the call within the cord's reach (`CORD_REACH`, 1.9 m from the phone, via `FirstPerson.constrain`); the handset parks off-hook on the desk once the call hangs up. A doorway collider blocks boarding until the call ends, and the descent state independently gates closure on the ended call. Repeat visitors can hang up with E after the first line.
- `src/descent/` owns sections 2–4: optional gaze-triggered floor lines, the movable car and the basement. `state.ts` changes the car origin to X=40 only behind closed doors, independently of dialogue readiness. The default ride is 22 seconds so its spoken lines finish before opening. Doors reopen for someone backing out. `room.ts` uses existing material IDs; `screens.ts` supplies scrolling CRTs and indicators; `audio.ts` shares the live/offline spatial mixer and fetches stems only after Begin/unlock. Subtitles advance on recorded durations even without audio; late decoded stems seek to the current subtitle clock. The far door fades to black without a title or an office scene. Descent settings use the shared Backquote panel.
- `shared/descent-lines.ts` preserves the script and inter-line edit pauses; `descent-recordings.json` holds processed timings and levels. `scripts/generate-descent.ts`, `process-descent.py` and `transcribe-descent.ts` author and inspect the 11 authorized v4 stems without playback. Raw takes remain in the story gallery; only processed stems ship. Phone stems target −39 dBFS RMS and speaker stems −34 dBFS RMS before runtime attenuation.
- Physical WASD/arrows move, mouse looks after a click, E answers/hangs up, F toggles the torch, M mutes. Audio starts only after a canvas click and is muted while unfocused.
- `bun run check` runs TypeScript, local import-cycle detection, focused tests and a production build. `bun run check:shaders` validates all seven generated shaders with Naga.
- `scripts/capture.ts --input` runs headless and muted through HiveMind, captures the two cameras, and checks pickup/hangup, physical Dvorak movement, flashlight and panel ownership. Use `--fetch captures/nightshift` to retrieve its output.
- `scripts/compare.py` produces a two-view concept/capture sheet, 4× difference images, RGB MAE, linear luminance, hue, histograms and an 8×6 grid. The concepts and scene are not pixel-registered; metrics describe differences rather than perceptual similarity.
- `src/props.ts` loads generated period props and credited CC0/CC-BY models through shared glTF/physical-light/shadow passes. `public/CREDITS.md` records sources, licences and adaptations. `scripts/prepare-generated.ts RAW_DIR OUTPUT_DIR [id]` keeps source geometry, converts the maps to 1K WebP and separates the CRT phosphor glass; preserve raw files outside the repository. `scripts/preview-props.py` renders three neutral angles headlessly. Export into a separate artifact directory, inspect the three-angle review, then copy the GLBs and `authored.json` into `public/props/authored/`. Keep the editable blends in the gallery. Use `--python-exit-code 1` so Blender script failures fail the job.
- `scripts/answer-video.ts` uses deterministic GPU readback, then composites the actual subtitle text and muxes the saved call without playback. Avoid screenshot/stability waits against its frozen RAF clock. Original captures and generated paint-over targets remain distinct in comparisons (`compare.py --paintover`).
