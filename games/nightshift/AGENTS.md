# Night Shift ’87

- Unlisted first-person cubicle office at `/arcade/nightshift/`, rendered with `@arcade/kit`. Keep `noindex` and omit a landing panel.
- `src/world.ts` owns metre-scale procedural geometry, colliders and the wide/phone comparison cameras. Eye height is 1.65 m and vertical FOV is 68°.
- `src/shaders.ts` composes physical lighting, spot-map shadows, analytic blind gobos, procedural surface recipes and shared fog. Shared screen-space contact shadows run before fog (Shadows panel; omitted under `?smoke`, as in Lot). Derivatives belong at fragment entry, before divergent material branches.
- `src/settings.ts` exposes lighting, air, movement, audio and handset travel through the shared Backquote panel.
- `shared/call.ts` owns ringing → pickup → call → hangup → ended. `src/audio.ts` synthesizes bells/hum and plays `public/audio/jay-call.mp3` at the subtitle clock. Kit authorized his ElevenLabs v4 voice as fictional Jay. `scripts/generate-call.ts` and `process-call.py` reproduce the stems/processing without playback. After hangup the far-right corner office is reachable; its encounter is a stub.
- Physical WASD/arrows move, mouse looks after a click, E answers/hangs up, F toggles the torch, M mutes. Audio starts only after a canvas click and is muted while unfocused.
- `bun run check` runs TypeScript, local import-cycle detection, focused tests and a production build. `bun run check:shaders` validates all seven generated shaders with Naga.
- `scripts/capture.ts --input` runs headless and muted through HiveMind, captures the two cameras, and checks pickup/hangup, physical Dvorak movement, flashlight and panel ownership. Use `--fetch captures/nightshift` to retrieve its output.
- `scripts/compare.py` produces a two-view concept/capture sheet, 4× difference images, RGB MAE, linear luminance, hue, histograms and an 8×6 grid. The concepts and scene are not pixel-registered; metrics describe differences rather than perceptual similarity.
- `src/props.ts` loads generated period props and credited CC0/CC-BY models through shared glTF/physical-light/shadow passes. `public/CREDITS.md` records sources, licences and adaptations. `scripts/prepare-generated.ts RAW_DIR OUTPUT_DIR [id]` keeps source geometry, converts the maps to 1K WebP and separates the CRT phosphor glass; preserve raw files outside the repository. `scripts/preview-props.py` renders three neutral angles headlessly. Partition rails must sit proud of fabric and follow the long axis; coplanar trim caused the moving patches. Noise octaves and bump normals fade by pixel footprint.
- The receiver's support span and rest position must fit the actual base. `tests/cradle.test.ts` checks both supports against the shipped GLB. The fitted roll also drives the cord endpoint. Keep one moving receiver.
- `scripts/author-hard-surface.py` rebuilds the CRT, receiver-free phone base and rolodex in headless Blender (metres, Z-up/-Y-front; glTF Y-up/+Z-front). Export into a separate artifact directory, inspect the three-angle review, then copy the GLBs and `authored.json` into `public/props/authored/`. Keep the editable blends in the gallery. Use `--python-exit-code 1` so Blender script failures fail the job.
- `scripts/answer-video.ts` uses deterministic GPU readback, then composites the actual subtitle text and muxes the saved call without playback. Avoid screenshot/stability waits against its frozen RAF clock. Original captures and generated paint-over targets remain distinct in comparisons (`compare.py --paintover`).
- Walking: `@arcade/kit/client/walk` owns the distance/contact clock and critically damped presentation; shared `walk-camera` applies it after collision movement. The local adapters retain chair contact and office creaks. `@arcade/kit/audio/footsteps` owns the quiet seeded contact PCM/scheduler; `foley.ts` owns room tone and the live/offline room mix. Keep contacts independent of camera-motion settings, and use actual displacement, not requested velocity, so walls don't create footsteps. `WALK-NOTES.md` records sources and dimensions. `scripts/walk-trace.ts` audits 30/60/120/144 Hz; `walk-video.ts` records actual input and muxes an offline sound tape without speaker playback.
- Storage: `stackContacts` records generated mesh ranges. Compute stack heights from support geometry, and keep `tests/stacks.test.ts` checking the packed triangles (not just the height metadata). Cabinet support is its shell, not protruding handles/drawer fronts.
- Office polish: `src/polish/` owns shadowed dust, the shared tube/audio signal, lens, props and off-frustum continuity. `POLISH-NOTES.md` records controls, proof and cost. Use actual displacement for dust/paper, immutable rest vertices for moving buffers, and swept frustum bounds plus last-seen delay for continuity. `scripts/polish-proof.ts` records muted Metal frames and offline Foley; `--boot` checks startup, `--perf` records only GPU timings. Smoke keeps static geometry and omits the new pipelines.
