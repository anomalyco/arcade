Wings — style sheet options

Each option is one consistent system: a fixed palette with a job for every color, one typeface, one material treatment, and rules for what's allowed. Pick one (or mix specific parts) and everything in the game gets locked to it: world, aircraft, effects, UI and HUD.

A · Clean Summer closest to Fortnite

Bright, clean, confident. Natural blues and greens carry the world; the UI is deep navy and white with one accent (sunny yellow). Colors are saturated but never neon. Everything is smooth and glossy, like a polished toy.
