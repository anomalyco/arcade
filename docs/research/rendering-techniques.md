# Rendering Techniques

Catalogue of real-time rendering techniques for Arcade's top-down and three-quarter WebGPU games (`@arcade/kit`, Outbreak, Blackout, Getaway, Carrier), synthesised from:

- **Arcade** (`packages/kit/gpu/`, `packages/kit/fx/`, `packages/kit/textures/`, `docs/research/game-notes.md`),
- **Kit's OpenCode 2.0 blog game ("The Open Plan")** (`src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlan*.ts`, `src/flashlight.wgsl`), and
- **Lamplight** (`docs/rendering-tricks.md`, `docs/game-techniques.md`, `docs/background-lighting.md`, `docs/hardware-target.md`, `docs/smoke-and-shutter.md`).

Proven per-pass measurements and benchmark logs live in [`docs/research/game-notes.md`](game-notes.md) and `perf/*.md`; this document catalogues the techniques, their current status in Arcade, and how to apply them. What reads as real, by topic, with Kit's decisions and the pitfalls, is in [realism.md](../realism.md).

- **Status key:**
  - **have** — Shipped in `@arcade/kit` or one of the workspace games (file path cited).
  - **partial** — Shipped in one game or Lab section, or simplified compared to the full technique.
  - **missing** — Not yet implemented in Arcade.
- **Cost convention:** Measured or estimated GPU time on Apple Silicon (M4/M5 Max Metal) at `1000×680`–`3456×1894` unless noted otherwise.

### Parked storm-cloud experiment (22 October 2026)

D-59 keeps Today's storm. `weather-clouds-parked` preserves the experiment at `1f439633` and its
`perf/weather-realism.md` ledger. The noise-volume density model produced soft, mottled ceilings rather than deep
overlapping billows, torn edges and a compact illuminated core behind dark foreground cloud. Fourteen rendered
candidates, including scud isolation and 128-step sampling, did not solve that structural limit. A later attempt
should try a different cloud-structure technique rather than more density or sampling sweeps.

### Entries — Lighting & GI

- **Windowed inverse-square + cone practical lights:** Evaluates diffuse and specular in a single loop over culled lights (`lightSum` in `packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts:352`), rejecting on squared plan distance `dPlan2 >= r * r` before `sqrt`, clamping near-fixture distance `dn = max(d3, max(hl, 1.25))` so wall tops and desks directly under a bulb never blow out hotter than the floor, and shaping spotlights with a smooth cubic cone feather. **Why it matters:** Keeps top-down rooms readable without hot-spotting wall caps near the camera. **Status:** **have** (`packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts:352`, `games/getaway/src/render/shaders.ts:83`).
- **CPU + frustum + conservative-bounds light culling:** Culls map lights against the camera ground frustum (`cam.groundRect` + clip planes `inLightFrustum`), partitions shadow-casting lights into a contiguous prefix (`shadowLightCount`), and bounds shadowless suffixes (e.g. Blackout's desktop monitors) with a plan AABB (`F.monitorBounds`) so pixels outside the box skip the suffix entirely. **Why it matters:** Top-down maps have 30–100+ practical lights across a town or office; culling off-screen lamps cut Outbreak interior lights from 28 to 11 (`-3.39 ms` GPU). **Status:** **have** (`games/blackout/src/render/lighting.ts:82`, `games/outbreak/src/main.ts:739`). Cross-link: [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu).
- **Spatial 2D light-list grid (`uLampGrid`):** Precomputes a 2D plan texture (`0.5 m` cells, `192×192` `rgba8uint`) on the CPU where each texel stores the 1-based indices of up to 4 static lamps whose radius and room/wall/cone mask overlap that cell (`packages/kit/gpu/lamp-grid.ts`), while dynamic lights (flashlights, muzzle flashes, vehicle lights) stay in the direct prefix `[firstLight .. directLightCount)`. **Why it matters:** Eliminates the per-pixel loop over all frustum-visible static lamps in `lightSum`; `sampleLampGrid` packs the 4 `u8` candidates into a single `u32` scalar before the loop (keeping texture locals out of the `lightSum` loop to preserve Apple Silicon SIMD occupancy) and exits on the first zero byte. **Status:** **have** (`packages/kit/gpu/lamp-grid.ts`, `packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts`, `packages/kit/gpu/renderer.ts`, `games/outbreak/src/main.ts`). **Cost:** `144 KiB` (`192×192×4 B`), dirty-row uploaded only when static lamp footprints or streaming `windowOrigin` change; paired in-page GPU Δ (`scripts/ab-gpu.ts`, 12 rounds at `3456×1894` on `mini`): `-0.230 ms` (MAD `0.056 ms`) street night (`6.557 → 6.283 ms`), `-0.315 ms` (MAD `0.614 ms`) wide street night (`7.470 → 7.150 ms`), `-0.435 ms` (MAD `0.015 ms`) interior night (`9.404 → 8.975 ms`), and `-0.617 ms` (MAD `0.034 ms`) 500-zombie night horde (`9.519 → 8.918 ms`), with `0.0000%` pixel diff (`maxChannelDiff = 0`, `rmse = 0`) on wide street, interior, and horde frames (`rmse = 0.161 / 255`, `0.0015%` pixels `> 1/255` on close street). **Source:** Blog (`src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanLampLighting.ts:50`), [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu).
- **Baked 2D indirect bounce flood-fill (`bounceTex`):** Flood-fills each static light's colour through open floor cells (`0.5 m` grid) using an 8-neighbour corner-safe Dijkstra search blocked by walls $\ge 1.8\text{ m}$ (`bakeBounce` in `games/blackout/src/render/level-mesh.ts:616`). Stores $\sqrt{\text{rgb}}$ in `.rgb` for dark-end precision and wall-free coverage in `.a` (`bounceAt` renormalises by `max(b.a, 0.25)` at `world.xz + n.xz * 0.3`). Linear irradiance arrays are retained on the CPU so intensity/day-night sliders recombine and upload without re-running Dijkstra. **Why it matters:** Lets lit rooms bleed warm colour through open doorways and around corridor corners instead of dropping to uniform grey ambient outside the direct cone. **Status:** **have** in Blackout (`games/blackout/src/render/level-mesh.ts`) and Outbreak (`bakeOutbreakBounce` in `games/outbreak/src/lighting.ts`, which also bakes room lamps' line-of-sight pools from `lightbake.ts`). Cross-link: [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#art-direction).
- **Baked 2D floor contact AO (`floorTex.r`) & Multi-bounce AO (Jimenez):** Rasterises wall, window, tree, and furniture footprints into a 2D plan grid (`12.5 cm` in Blackout with two 7-tap box blurs; `25 cm` analytic exponential distance in Outbreak), sampled in `shadeScene` with vertical attenuation `low = 1.0 - smoothstep(0.0, 0.9, world.y)` and Jimenez's cubic multi-bounce polynomial so coloured carpets, wood, and grass retain saturated interreflection in crevices rather than turning muddy grey. **Why it matters:** Grounds every wall base, table, sofa, and tree trunk in top-down view with zero per-frame SSAO pass cost. **Status:** **have** (`games/blackout/src/render/level-mesh.ts:497`, `games/outbreak/src/level.ts:529`, `packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts:665`).
- **Baked 3D vertex AO & bevel tone (`aOpenTone`):** Encodes per-vertex contact darkening at object bases (`bottomAo = 0.45..0.55`), bright edge highlights on upper chamfers (`topBoost = 1.18..1.22`), and soft Gaussian floor footprints under props directly into a vertex attribute during procedural mesh construction (`openPlanModels.ts:59–196, 1407`). **Why it matters:** 2D plan-space `floorTex.r` cannot distinguish the underside of a table top, the inside of a sink basin, a recessed window jamb, or an upper cabinet bevel from the floor below it; vertex tone gives crisp 3D Playdead-style form on every prop for zero runtime cost. **Status:** **partial** — `packages/kit/gpu/foliage.ts` bakes root/canopy AO into `v.color.rgb` and Outbreak stamps tone into `v.color.a` (`stampOutbreakVertexTone`), but the kit's `Mesh.box`/`bevelBox` (`packages/kit/gpu/mesh.ts`) leave it at `1.0`. **Source:** Blog (`src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanModels.ts`).
- **Analytical rectangular area-light floor bounce (`officeFloorBounce`):** Given the room-space rectangle `uLitFloor = vec4(x0, x1, z0, z1)` where sunlight or window light lands on the floor, clamps any surface point's `xz` to `uLitFloor` at floor height to find the nearest lit floor point and evaluates `lowRoom * (facing * 0.85 + 0.15) / (1.0 + d2 * 0.6)` (`openPlanAtmosphere.ts:22`). **Why it matters:** Gives instant, noise-free 1-bounce floor-to-wall and floor-to-character illumination around window shafts and porch pools in 6 ALU ops. **Status:** **missing** in Arcade. **Source:** Blog (`src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanAtmosphere.ts:22`), Lamplight (`docs/rendering-tricks.md` §1.10).
- **Analytical clipped rectangular area light & rough specular sheen (`openGlassLight` / `openGlassSheen`):** Integrates the exact Lambertian form factor of a rectangular emitter (`openGlassEdge` rational approximation) clipped against its reveal/sill horizon (`hwGlassFloor`), plus a solid-angle-weighted rough specular lobe (`openGlassSheen`) and soft planar window reflection (`hwDeskWindow`, blurring `clerestoryOpen` by `0.035 + t * rough² * 0.16`). **Why it matters:** Replaces circular point-light hotspots at windows and TV/monitor screens with true rectangular light throws and stretched window reflections across floors and wet driveways. **Status:** **missing** in Arcade (Outbreak approximates window pools with a `rx²·ry²` point-light mask in `games/outbreak/src/shaders.ts:21`). **Source:** Blog (`src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanHardware.ts:113–284`), [Heitz et al. 2016](https://eheitzresearch.wordpress.com/415-2/).
- **Analytical window cookie + canopy dapple (`sunCookie` / `clerestoryCanopy`):** Projects world points along the sun/moon vector to the window plane or overhead tree canopy, box-filtering panes and mullions over a kernel width `w = w0 + throwDist * spread` (sharp near the sill, soft across the room) and multiplying by wind-advected multi-scale canopy/cloud waves. **Why it matters:** Makes outdoor streets, lawns, and indoor window patches feel alive with moving leaf dapple and contact-hardening mullion shadows without high-res shadow cascades. **Status:** **partial** — Blackout bakes a static `3.125 cm` R8 window mask (`games/blackout/src/render/level-mesh.ts:547`); Outbreak stamps static `3 m` interior window boxes (`games/outbreak/src/level.ts:544`). **Source:** Blog (`src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanSun.ts:57`), Lamplight (`docs/rendering-tricks.md` §1.6), [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu).
- **L1 Spherical Harmonics / azimuthal sky ambient:** Projects the time-of-day sky dome (cool zenith, warm solar-azimuth horizon, ground bounce) into L1/L2 Spherical Harmonics (or 3D probe grid `BakedLighting` in Lamplight) so shadowed normals facing the sunset/moon catch warm/cool directional fill while downward normals catch ground bounce. **Why it matters:** Prevents flat, uniform ambient on vertical walls and character silhouettes when out of direct light. **Status:** **missing** (`ambientAt(n)` in `packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts:452` is 1D vertical `n.y` only). **Source:** Lamplight (`docs/rendering-tricks.md` §1.7, `docs/background-lighting.md:83`).

- **Physical lights and surfaces (`lights/physical.ts`):** scene hooks a game spreads into its own (`withPhysicalLights(game, opts)` composes them). Per light: windowed inverse-square from the fixture's 3D position, `(1 − (d/r)⁴)² / max(d², s²)` with the fixture radius `s` as the near clamp, and real 3D spot cones (`SceneLight.spot` packs `cone = (axis.xz, cosOuter, 3 + cosInner)`, axis pointing down or level) with an optional spill floor; then GGX with height-correlated Smith (Hammon's form) and Schlick, the Fresnel split so F0 applies after the loop (`spec = F0·x + y`), and Karis' sphere-light widening `α' = α + s/2d` energy-kept so a fixture mirrors as a patch, not a pin-prick. Geometric specular AA widens α² by the screen derivatives of the normal (`min(2·0.25·(|∂n/∂x|² + |∂n/∂y|²), 0.18)`, computed at fragment entry in uniform control flow). The sky reflection is split-sum along Lagarde's dominant direction with his specular occlusion, using a least-squares environment BRDF in `(r, r², n·v, exp2(−9.28 n·v))` fitted to an importance-sampled reference (worst 0.06, RMS 0.015 for roughness 0.1–1; Karis' mobile fit is off by up to 0.4 for metals). The radiance it mirrors is a hook (`env`): the kit hemisphere by default, `mix(fogEnv(dir), fogAmbient(n), rough²)` with the fog port. Roughness comes from the material's `pbrRough`/`pbrMetal` or the legacy shininess via `α² = 2/(n + 2)`. Light units: `power × GAIN` is intensity, so a diffuse surface gets `albedo · I · cosθ / (π d²)` as Lot's hook already did. **Status:** opt-in; default shaders are byte-identical (checked); the Lab's World section has a Light model row and a "Night lamps, physical" preset (its lights convert by matching Today straight under each fixture). **Why it matters:** first-person scenes with Ashwood's PBR assets read as wet asphalt, car paint and metal under the teal sky instead of Blinn hot spots. **Proof:** `packages/kit/gpu/lights/physical.test.ts` (GGX normalisation, env BRDF vs reference, falloff, naga), `scripts/light-gpu-proof.ts` (Deno/Metal), `scripts/lab/prove-physical-lights.ts` (stills + paired GPU). **Cost** (paired in-page GPU, `scripts/lab/prove-physical-lights.ts`, 12 rounds at 3456×1894 on `mini`, Lab night lamps): +0.11 ms street level (MAD 0.03; 4.44 → 4.54 ms), +0.12 to +0.21 ms three-quarter (MAD 0.05–0.11; 4.78 → 4.88–5.00 ms).
- **Stateless lamp flicker and failure (`lights/flicker.ts`):** `lampFlicker(kind, seed, time, amount)` is a pure function, so co-op clients, replays and paired captures agree and frame rate can't change it. Sodium cycles burn → sag → dark (warmth 0) → restrike that blinks then warms pink → orange (`coldSodiumTint`), and only some cycles fail (more with `amount`); fluorescent tubes hum, then per hashed time window either stutter at ~60 ms toggles or drop out with 2–4 starter blinks and an ignite ramp; incandescent browns out; beacons pulse. Lab › Look and world › "Dying lamps" shows all three practicals.
- **Spot shadow-map pool (`shadows/spot-shadows.ts`):** polar plan shadows are cheap and omnidirectional for many lamps seen from above but can't see overhangs, canopies, car undersides or fences; a first-person view needs those for the two or three lights nearest the camera. `SpotShadowPicker` scores spots within `range + 15 m` by `intensity / max(d − 0.3·range, 1)²` (nominal intensity, so flicker doesn't reshuffle), keeps holders with a 1.6× bonus and breaks ties by id; slots cross-fade over 0.35 s (instant after a >1.5 m camera jump) and follow stable `SceneLight.id`s, found in the packed frame with `SceneLights.indexOf`. `SpotShadows` renders a `depth32float` 2D-array layer per slot with a position-only pipeline over the game's static vertex buffers, only when a slot changes hands, its light moves or `invalidate()` says the world changed. The scene samples it in the light loop (`physicalLightHooks({ spotShadows: true })` multiplies `att` by `spotShadowVis(i, world, n)`): normal offset of 0.6 texels, then a depth slack of `texel · (1.2·tanθ + 0.6) · bias` converted to NDC, and a fixed 3×3 grid of hardware-filtered comparison taps (no per-pixel noise, so nothing crawls under TAA). **Status:** opt-in, Physical only; Lab preset "Spot shadow maps" (streetlamp and porch). Polar shadows stay on for the same lights (they agree where both see the caster). **Proof:** `spot-shadows.test.ts` (picker hysteresis, fades, dirty rules, view-projection maps the cone into NDC, uniform packing); `deno run -A --sloppy-imports scripts/light-gpu-proof.ts 0` renders it on Metal with no validation errors, the pole and character shadows appear and only 2e-6 of pixels brighten. **Cost** (same harness, steady state, maps already rendered): +0.37 ms with two slots at street level (MAD 0.04–0.07; 4.54 → 4.93 ms), +0.10 to +0.25 ms with one slot from the three-quarter camera. A slot change also renders one 1024² depth layer of the static world (22.6k vertices in the Lab). Changing the slot count snaps maps in; a light that comes into range later fades in over 0.35 s.
- **Shared torch beam profile (`lights/beam.ts`):** Outbreak's flashlight intensity distribution (the default path of `flashProfile`) as a pure function of t = angle / half-angle: `(1 − smoothstep(inner, 1, t)) · rings + core · L(t / coreWidth) + spill · L(t / spillWidth)`, with `L(x) = 1 / (1 + x²(1 + 0.48x²))`, reflector rings as two bells (bright at t 0.84, dark just outside the core) and nothing past `1.7 · spillWidth`. It beats a plain `smoothstep(cosOuter, cosInner)` spot (Aiden's) for a handheld torch: the core reads as the bulb and the spill keeps the ground beside the beam from going black. `SceneLight.beam = { axis, halfAngle }` packs `cone = (axis.x, axis.z, axis.y, 5 + halfAngle)`, so a torch can point up; `pbrCone` shapes those lights with the profile and keeps the physical distance falloff (Outbreak's near-lens softening `1/(1 + d²/36)` is a top-down readability choice and stays there). One profile per scene (`physicalLightHooks({ beam: { a, b } })`, `packBeamProfile`), Darkwood by default; `HOT_CORE_BEAM` is the v7 values on the same path. Lab › Look and world › Physical › Flashlight beam. **Proof:** `beam.test.ts` (core on axis, monotone outside the cone, zero past the spill, Darkwood flat vs hot core peaky, packing), naga on a composed shader with a beam uniform, and `LIGHT_PROOF_FLASHLIGHT=1 deno run -A --sloppy-imports scripts/light-gpu-proof.ts 0` (Metal, no validation errors).
- **One-bounce plan-space light (`gi/plan-bounce.ts`, AR-115):** fire, torches, muzzle flashes, lamps and lit windows spill their colour onto nearby floors and walls. Each frame, after the polar pass, three compute stages run on a 64 m grid around the camera's ground focus (texel 0.25/0.5/1 m, origin snapped to the coarsest of 4 mips). **Emission:** every texel takes the light the frame's lights put on it (the game's attenuation hook, Outbreak's `outbreakPlanBounceShader`), shadowed by the polar map for shadow-casting lights and by a short march through a CPU-rastered occluder height map (`rasterPlanOccupancy`, re-uploaded only when walls or the window change) for the rest, times a floor or wall reflectance; alpha is how much the texel blocks. **Mips:** a 2×2 box chain. **Gather:** 16 cones per texel march out, reading the mip whose texel matches the cone's width and losing what walls block; the inverse-square kernel with a soft core is normalised so a uniformly lit neighbourhood returns its own leaving light, then `strength` scales it. Scenes read it with `planBounceAt`, which weights the four nearest texels by how open they are, so light never bleeds through a wall. **Why it matters:** colour comes back only where light lands (Outbreak feeds the luminance into its colour rule via `outbreakGiIrr`), and the cost scales with the grid, not the light count. Nothing is jittered or accumulated, so it holds still under TAA and is the same for one torch or six. It is the cheap cone-traced cousin of one radiance-cascade level, enough for one bounce over a few metres. **Status:** on by default in Outbreak at ×3 (Kit picked it in D-47), Today one Lab setting away. Physically normalised ×1 reads faintly after Outbreak's grade, hence ×3. Null input, zero strength or a frame with no lights (daylight) skips every pass, and the pipelines compile only on the first frame that needs them. **Proof:** `plan-bounce.test.ts` (window snapping, rasterisation, packing, naga), `games/outbreak/tests/bounce-light.test.ts`, stills and a hall-sweep clip from `games/outbreak/scripts/bounce-stills.ts`. **Cost:** paired `ab-gpu.ts` on `pro` (Metal, 3456×1894, 24 rounds, 22:00 street and Ash interior, 18:00 street): Δ −0.02 to +0.04 ms (MAD 0.04–0.08 night, 0.18–0.40 dusk) at 0.5 m and 0.25 m, inside the run's noise; SwiftShader (CI's adapter) not yet measured. The renderer's frame timer now starts at the bounce pass when there is no polar pass, so these timings include it.
- **Street lamps as lens emitters (`lights/street.ts`):** Kit: "you can tell the light comes from a single point beneath." A cobra head's refractor glows across a ~0.6 × 0.3 m bowl and throws a wide, asymmetric pool, so the lamp is modelled as that: (1) **photometry** from the pool it is designed to light — an even ellipse on the ground with half its peak at `along`, `street` and `house` mounting heights (IES Type II–III, short), turned into candela by `I = E / cos³θ` (the batwing, peak ~50° off nadir, 1.2 MH along the road), a 62° refractor cutoff and a faint side glow; `streetFluxGain` scales it to the round spot's flux × `flux`; (2) **lens-kernel falloff** `1/(d² + b²)` (on-axis disc irradiance: no hotspot under the head); (3) **highlights and wet glints** from the lens point nearest the mirror ray, α widened by the lens half-width (Karis), and fog glints widened by the lens's angular size; (4) **airlight from the lens**: equiangular samples about the lens point nearest the view ray with softened distance √(h² + b²), each weighted by the closed-form mean of `1/(d² + b²)` along the lens line, so the fan leaves the whole lens with no singular dot and no noise (CPU mirror `airlightLensEquiangular` within 15 % of the full 2-D lens integral off-grazing). The beam has its own phase (`phase`, g 0.1): with the scene's g 0.6, a camera standing in a lamp's pool sees the brightest air between it and the head, so the beam read as a ball round the head; at low g it reads as the wedge below the lens. A short visible reach (`air` 0.6) with more scattering (`haze` 4.2) keeps the wedge off the fog bank below; (4b) **sky glow**: a cutoff head sends no light up, so its share of the lamp-glow map (bank tint, cloud underglow) is the ground bounce, `FogLamp.uplight` = `uplight` 0.12. At full share the street lamps lit the whole cloud deck olive over the gate (sky 25,31,25 → 6,22,21 display RGB at the hero camera; the paint-over has 6,30,32, the rest of the gap is the cloud deck's own shade); (5) **PCSS** `spotShadowVisArea`: a 9-tap blocker search over the lens footprint, penumbra `lens·(d − d_b)/d_b`, 8 rotated Vogel comparison taps; (6) the **lens bowl** as geometry (`pushLens`, sag or flat glass, aux core → rim) shaded emissive by the game; on the cobra head it fills the housing's underside (0.64 × 0.24 m) in even amber, so the head reads as a glowing lens under a dark shell. The arm comes from the pole collider (`armFromPoles`). **Status:** Lot (booth cobra head, lot shoeboxes, road davit); lamps without a pole collider stay point spots. Outbreak has its own port as a Lab option (AR-124, below). **Proof:** `street.test.ts` (half-max reaches, batwing, cutoff, flux, arm, bowl), `fog-scatter.test.ts` (lens estimator vs reference, bounded at grazing, packing), naga via `games/lot/scripts/check-shaders.ts`; captures and paint-over target in `~/code/home/artifacts/lot-2026-10-20/lamps-*` with `lamps-research-2026-10-20.md`. **Cost:** see table; Today reproduces main within JPEG error. LTC disc lights were rejected for now: at 6–9 m the lens subtends < 3°, where LTC's exact diffuse is indistinguishable from the softened kernel; revisit for wall-mounted area fixtures near glossy surfaces.

- **BARBARA shadow pops (parked, October 2026):** Matched six-second walks on mini/Metal at 1600×900 High measured

  reduced excursions **14%** and the peak **32%** versus shipped Today, at roughly **+0.75 ms**. Follow-up probes
  used lens-only as their control, changing one layer at a time:

    changes; the effect was negligible. Texel-snapping the torch projection at a world anchor, with stable near/far
    planes, was also negligible.
  - Disabling visible wind to match the windless shadow geometry reduced excursions **8.1%**; this also removes
    genuine motion, so it is not a measured wind-matching fix.
  - Bypassing only TAA colour-history clipping reduced excursions **25%**. Bounded clipping fixes added at most

  **TAA history on shadow edges is the biggest remaining lever**, but retaining stale light can lower this score

- **GPU polar 1D ray-cast shadow map (`polarShader`):** Casts `POLAR_RAYS = 512` rays per culled light in a compute pass (`workgroup_size(64)`) against axis-aligned wall/furniture slabs and circular person casters. Stores the nearest full-height blocker (`full`) plus the two nearest partial-height blockers `(t0, t1, h)` per ray (`polarOut`), so low tables, counters, sofas, and window sills cast finite-length shadows (`partialShadow`) while walls cast infinite shadows. **Why it matters:** Gives omnidirectional point-light and cone-light shadows for up to 64 lights in a single compute pass without 64 cubemap render passes. **Status:** **have** (`packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts:63–343`). Cross-link: [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu).
- **Interpolated multi-tap polar visibility PCF:** Evaluates `tapVis` at adjacent integer rays `(lo, lo + 1)` and interpolates *visibility* (never blocker distance, which leaks across silhouettes) across a 5-tap (Outbreak) or 9-tap world-hashed (Blackout) angular kernel, reusing `prevVis` when `lo == prevLo`. **Why it matters:** Eliminates radial staircase bands on floors and tables without a fullscreen blur pass. **Status:** **have** (`packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts:293–336`). Cross-link: [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu).
- **Static CPU-rasterised radial depth atlas for local lamps (`openPlanLampLighting.ts`):** Rasterises opaque scene triangles once on the CPU (or in a Web Worker: `openPlanSceneWorker.ts`) into a `512×512` R32F radial-depth atlas (`16` tiles of `128×128`), sampled with 2×2 PCF (`officeLampVisibility`) for both surface pools and volumetric lamp air. **Why it matters:** Static room/porch/desk lamps never move; pre-rasterising their blocker depth once per seed eliminates per-frame shadow-caster gathering and compute ray-casting for static fixtures. **Status:** **missing** in Arcade. **Cost:** `+0.075 ms` paired frame delta on M2 Max (`perf/open-plan-lamps.md`); `1.01 MiB` texture. **Source:** Blog (`src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanLampLighting.ts:136`).
- **Texel-snapped orthographic directional shadow map + dirty-gated static cache:** Renders directional sun/moon casters into a `2048²` or `4096×H` depth texture with projection origin snapped to texel increments (`round(origin * res) / res`), culling chunks that neither intersect the camera frustum nor cast a shadow sweeping into the camera frustum (`aabbInFrustum` with `sweep` in `games/getaway/src/render/renderer.ts:551`). When the camera or sun is stationary (or static geometry is cached into `static_shadow_tex` as in Lamplight), static casters are not redrawn. **Why it matters:** Outbreak currently has **no directional sun/moon shadow map** (`docs/research/outbreak-look-v2.md:91`), so houses, fences, and trees cast no directional exterior shadows across the street or lawn. **Status:** **partial** — **have** in Getaway (`games/getaway/src/render/renderer.ts:534`); **missing** in Outbreak and Blackout. **Source:** [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu), Blog (`openPlanSun.ts:171`), Lamplight (`docs/rendering-tricks.md` §1.1).
- **Screen-space contact shadows (`contactShadowVolume`):** A half-resolution volume slot reads only the scene depth: the normal comes from the depth (per axis, the closer neighbour, so silhouettes don't smear), and each pixel marches 12 jittered steps (0.6 m) toward the one or two lights whose diffuse irradiance there is largest. A sample more than a depth-slope bias and less than 12 cm behind the stored depth is a blocker; samples within 2 px of the start are skipped (Bend's near-pixel rejection), which with the slope bias stops grazing walls shadowing themselves. Each light's share of the light at the pixel (its irradiance over the sum of every light's plus the fog sky's `fogAmbient`) is how much of the pixel it darkens, so a pixel lit mostly by the sky barely changes. The result multiplies the lit scene (`composite: "multiply"`, history 0.25, the depth-aware upsample). **Why it matters:** Shadow maps are too coarse for the last few centimetres: the pole foot, plinth edge, bollards and kerbs float without it. **Status:** **partial**, on in the Lot (Shadows › Contact shadows; Today one setting away, not compiled under `?smoke`). **Cost:** `+0.25–0.8 ms` at 1920×1080 on `pro` (five Lot poses, 72 warmed frames per side, low tail); SwiftShader at 640×360 (Lot boothNear, all three features on vs Today, Studio): ≈130–140 ms per frame either way, no measurable difference after the first compile round; CI smoke stays Today. **Source:** Bend Studio (*Days Gone*) as implemented by three.js r181 `SSSNode` (MIT); the WGSL is ours.
- **Outbreak ports of the Lot's lamps, mist, contact shadows and rooms (AR-124):** four Lab options in `games/outbreak/src/{street-lamps,mist,contact-shadows,window-rooms}/`, all four on by default since Kit's picks (D-49 lens lamps, D-50 mist, D-51 rooms, D-52 contact shadows), each Today one setting away. *Lens street lamps* reshape the resolved street lamps (`lens.ts`): the kit's `streetProfile` and `1/(d² + b²)` kernel in Outbreak's `lightAttenuation` (cone `(arm, flux gain, 7 + b)`; power stays in Today's units, so reflections, dust and smoke read the same lamp), the arm towards the carriageway from the 96 m road bands (the two hand-placed origin posts aim at the pool Today lights), flux matched to Today's ground flux × `slFlux` by quadrature, polar shadows softened by the lens size, and the air under the head lit from the lens line (`evalLampAir`). *Low mist* is the kit fog bank (`fogScatterVolume({ atmosphere, street, bankMask })`) knee-high and dense, pooled by its mask (`mist/wgsl.ts`): banked against house walls, in ground-fixed hollows across the yards (static 2D noise the billows drift through) and in pockets along the gutters, nothing on the open road, indoors or on roofs, and thin mist lies lower (the layer's top follows the mask). Where the mask is 0 the kit's `fogBankSigma` skips its noise, so pooling made the mist slightly cheaper (−0.05 to −0.12 ms paired against the old even layer). - **Analytical capsule & foot contact shadows:** Evaluates soft cone/ellipsoid occlusion from character feet and bodies against the ground plane in the surface shader (or draws height-faded ground decals like Getaway's `kind == 9` wheel-weighted car shadow in `games/getaway/src/render/shaders.ts:489` and the blog game's `hwDiskSkyShadow` / `dressingContact`). **Why it matters:** Grounds survivors, zombies, and small props in overcast or ambient-lit interiors where polar point lights are dim or absent. **Status:** **partial** — Getaway has car/tree/building grounding decals (`games/getaway/src/render/shaders.ts:489`); Outbreak uses kit capsule shadows by default (next entry); Blackout relies on round polar casters (`m.y > 0.5`). **Source:** Lamplight (`docs/rendering-tricks.md` §1.5, `docs/ruins-lighting-review.md`), Blog (`openPlanHardware.ts:307`).
- **Analytic soft capsule character shadows (`@arcade/kit/gpu/shadows/capsule-shadows`, AR-113):** The CPU packs each drawn rig as 14 bone capsules (a lying body as 6), picks each body's 4 strongest packed lights (skipping a light inside the body, like its own torch or flames, and cones that miss it) and lists bodies in a 32×32 plan grid over the view: the contact disc plus a swept plan shadow per light and for the sun. The surface shader (`capsulePrepare` once per fragment, only in listed cells) adds a contact term per capsule, cosθ·(r/l)² multiplied into baked AO, and a soft visibility per picked light: the closest approach `h` of the shadow ray to each capsule at distance `t`, as `h/t` against the light's angular radius θ = size/distance. The shadow is sharp at the feet, widens toward the tip and softens as the light comes closer. `SceneHooks.lightVisibility` multiplies it into that light only, so it exists only where the light lands and darkness stays dark. A body never shadows itself (boxy meshes poke out of their capsules). Round polar casters of kind 2 are "shaft only", so the flashlight's volumetric beam is still cut by bodies while surfaces take the soft shadow. **Status:** **have**, Outbreak's default since D-48 (Look · Character shadows; Today one setting away). It composes with plan bounce (`gpu/gi/plan-bounce`): bounce is added to ambient before AO, so the contact term also darkens bounced light under bodies. **Cost:** paired `+0.18 ms` (60-zombie horde), `+0.22 ms` (street lamp), `+0.23 ms` (day) at 3456×1894 Metal, MADs ≤ 0.13; CPU `0.1–0.5 ms`. SwiftShader (1280×720, 4 rounds) runs ~5.6 s per frame either way (5568 vs 5698 ms, paired MAD 2.2 s), unresolvable, so CI smoke keeps Today (the default). **Source:** Íñigo Quílez's sphere/capsule soft-shadow and occlusion articles (technique only).

### Entries — Materials & Textures

- **Footprint-filtered (`fwidth`) procedural material recipes & Specular AA:** Computes world-space pixel footprint `surfaceFootprint = max(length(dpdx(o.world)), length(dpdy(o.world)))` in uniform control flow at fragment entry (`packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts:695`), band-limits procedural seams (`seam`) and grain (`grain`) toward their analytic mean before Nyquist aliasing, and folds unresolved micro-normal variance into roughness via `roughnessToGloss` (`effRough = sqrt(rough² + normalVariance * clamp(surfaceFootprint * 12, 0, 1))`, with `effRough >= 0.72` clamping broad specular to zero). **Why it matters:** Prevents sub-pixel shingle/plank/cobble shimmer during camera pan and avoids WGSL non-uniform derivative errors on Metal. **Status:** **have** (`packages/kit/gpu/surface-materials.ts:536–955`). Cross-link: [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu).
- **Generated flat-diffuse WebP texture array + linear-light mips + procedural breakup:** Prepares 16 `256×256` RGBA WebP textures (`1,098 KB` total) from unlit orthographic `gpt-image-1.5` candidates using mirrored-quadrant wrapping for isotropic surfaces, toroidal raised-cosine blending for horizontal courses (wood planks, brick, clapboard, shingles), and bilateral symmetry for area rugs, with a 9-level CPU linear-light mip chain (`buildLinearMipChain`) and authored roughness in alpha. In WGSL, `sampleMaterialFast` uses **compile-time constant array layer indices** per material branch and runs in `postLightMaterial` *after* `lightSum`. **Why it matters:** Dynamic `texture_2d_array` layer indexing across divergent branches stalled Apple Silicon SIMD groups (`41.8 ms → 24.2 ms`); moving material/texture evaluation after `lightSum` so texture locals don't overlap the 36-register shadow loop cut Outbreak interior GPU time to `14.47 ms`. **Status:** **have** (`packages/kit/textures/`, `packages/kit/gpu/surface-materials.ts`). Cross-link: [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu).
- **Local-space triplanar wear projection:** Projects a small unlit wear/patina texture in object-local coordinates using tight power-4 normal weights `w = pow(abs(n), vec3(4.0)) / dot(pow(abs(n), vec3(4.0)), vec3(1.0))` to modulate albedo, $F_0$, and roughness together (`openPlanHardware.ts:400`, `openPlanHardwareFinishes.ts:25`, Lamplight `docs/hardware-target.md`). **Why it matters:** Outbreak's interior furniture (`sofa`, `bed`, `table`, `fridge`, `stove`, `counter`) and parked car still use flat vertex colours; triplanar wear modulation gives 3D props tactile edge rubs, enamel chips, and fabric grain without UV unwrapping. **Status:** **missing** on props/vehicles in Arcade. **Source:** Blog (`openPlanHardware.ts:395`), Lamplight (`docs/hardware-target.md`).
- **Generated vertex-coloured prop meshes:** TRELLIS.2 turns one product image per prop into a textured GLB (71 s per asset on the Studio); Blender drops the hidden inner shells, turns inward faces out and decimates the TRELLIS mesh itself to two LOD budgets (an octree remesh only where nothing else fits, picked per LOD by surface distance to the source), samples the texture into per-vertex colour filtered to triangle size, picks `MAT.lit`/`MAT.shiny` per face from metallic/roughness and bakes AO into a separate byte, written as a compact `.prop` (14-byte vertices). The game draws them through the existing instanced `addModel` path, one draw per prop and LOD, LOD1 past a zoom threshold. **Why it matters:** real furniture silhouettes and wood grain in the box-built houses with no UVs, no new bindings and no texture memory; brightness, AO strength and saturation stay live knobs (`Renderer.setModel`). **Status:** **have**, on by default in Outbreak's start houses (Backquote › Props › "Furnish start houses" turns it off; the Low tier draws LOD1 only). **Source:** `scripts/props/README.md`.
- **Swept-point triangle-clipped surface splatter decals:** Traces ballistic droplets against packed visible receiver triangles (`splatter-surfaces.ts`) and clips each matte/glossy deposit in the fragment shader against up to 12 coplanar receiver triangles (`packages/kit/fx/splatter.ts`), preventing wall hits from bleeding onto floors or across thin partitions. **Why it matters:** Keeps combat blood/ink deposits crisp on top-down walls, furniture tops, and floors without projection-box decal overdraw. **Status:** **have** (`packages/kit/fx/splatter.ts`, `games/outbreak/src/combat-fx.ts`). Cross-link: [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu).
- **Thin glass (`thinGlassHooks`):** A blended pass over the game's physical lights treats a pane as a thin non-absorbing slab: reflectance `2R/(1+R)` of Schlick's `R` (IOR 1.52 gives ≈ 8 % head-on), and that is also the pane's opacity, so it is nearly clear face-on and a mirror at grazing. The reflection is the game's own environment and lamps through the physical path. A procedural layer in pane metres (two-octave smudges, dust at the frame, a ragged condensation band rising from the sill with drips cut through it) raises roughness, dims what shows through, and scatters light toward the eye from both sides (a second light sum with `-n`, glass pixels only). The pane's frame, size and identity come from screen derivatives of world position and its 0..1 UVs, so any quad works; its local position is read from the interpolated UV, because the centre is rebuilt per 2×2 quad and its rounding showed as quad-sized speckle. The game's fog hook runs twice so the in-scatter in front of the pane is not counted twice through the blend; faces looking into a lit room reflect that room (`roomSide`) instead of the sky. **Why it matters:** Kit: "you can't see through the glass in ours, but in his you can." **Status:** **partial**, on in the Lot (Glass › Glass). **Cost:** within noise at 1920×1080 on `pro`.
- **Interior-mapped windows (`interiorWindowHooks`):** Each lit pane intersects the view ray with a box behind it (van Dongen 2008) and re-projects the exit point onto one one-point-perspective room image (three-fenestra's `imRoomBoxUV`, MIT). Our atlas holds 8 rooms of a 1980s research facility (desk, office, lab, fume hood, CRT bank, workshop, beacon, conference) × 2 variants, ray-traced on the CPU by `scripts/textures/paint-rooms.ts` in ~5 s, so it is licence-clean; each cell carries a gain so far windows keep the flat pane's mean brightness. Per-vertex `aux = kind·4 + brightness`; blinds, 3-light mullions with a transom, the front glass and grime are drawn over it. The Lot bakes this as a separate `windows` stream 3 cm proud of Ashwood's window proxies, so Today is exactly what it was. **Why it matters:** lit windows read as rooms with depth and parallax instead of flat glowing rectangles. **Status:** **partial**, on in the Lot (Glass › Lit windows). **Cost:** `+0.1–0.3 ms` at 1920×1080 on `pro`.
- **Analytic wet-ground light reflections:** From Outbreak's steep top-down camera the things a puddle would reflect (lamp heads, lit windows, fire) are mostly off screen, so screen-space reflections would miss them and a planar pass would cost a second scene render. Instead the CPU picks up to 8 outdoor lights nearest the view (`packReflectionLights`: streetlamps, porch and window lights, fire, police lights, headlights; never table lamps, sconces or lights inside a house) and passes their slots in one `vec4f`. The ground shader draws each light's mirror image with the same mirror-point math as the kit's floor-plane highlight (`k = hl / (hl + eye.y)`, image pulled toward the eye): a tight image in standing water and a long soft streak on wet paving, cut by the light's cone. The survivor and the two nearest zombies stand in front of the lights behind them as dark reflected figures, the flashlight's hotspot is mirrored too, and raindrop ripples jitter the lookup. The loop never touches the shadow map, to keep register pressure off the main lights loop. Only road and paving ground kinds and flat shiny props outdoors take it (matte blood, litter and paint never shine); the street potholes always hold water, and the weather's puddles (`weather/puddles.ts`, `outbreakPuddleMask`) or the Lab's wetness (the same mask at its own level) add pools. **Why it matters:** wet night streets read from far off at almost no cost, and it only appears where there is light. **Status:** **have** (Outbreak, Look › Reflections; Today is `reflections` 0). **Cost:** first build, paired at 3456×1894 on `pro`: `+2.22 ms` (±0.03) on a wet night street with fire and Lab wetness 0.7, when the loop still read the light buffer per light; the light images then moved to the CPU (a distance test per light in the shader), which is not yet re-measured.
- **Analytic mirror glass:** A leaning floor mirror's glass (`MAT_MIRROR`, glass uv in vertex colour) computes its reflection in its own fragment shader with no extra pass: the reflected view ray hits the floor plane, which is shaded from the wood albedo and the baked bounce, sky and floor AO; detail 1 adds the flashlight beam on that floor, detail 2 ray-tests cylinders for the survivor and the two nearest zombies and adds the flashlight's lens flare when it is aimed into the glass. Ageing is analytic too: a wavy old plate, foxing, silver worn at the edges, grime the beam lights up and cracked shards. The mirror model is drawn instanced only when the camera's ground rect reaches it (max 8). **Why it matters:** a mirror that shows the room and the survivor passing, for the cost of the pixels it covers. **Status:** **have** (Outbreak: always in the start house, 14 % of other houses, 45 % of those cracked). **Cost:** first build, paired at 3456×1894 on `pro`: `+0.90 ms` (±0.21) for Ash's interior at night with reflections and the mirror on (cause not isolated; the mirror covers ~10k pixels); not yet re-measured after the CPU-image change.
- **Screen-space reflections and the wet/puddle term (Ashwood port):** Kit compared the Lot's road with Ashwood's: brightness already matched, but the Lot's road had 22–34 % less fine vertical detail, because Ashwood has two things the Lot didn't. First, a material wet term (`wet()`): two world-space noise octaves (41 m, 13 m) plus AO cavities pick puddles (roughness 0.035, flat normal, albedo ×0.55); a porous film darkens and smooths everything else, with drying streaks and sheen patches. The kit port (`gpu/reflections/wet.ts`, `wetSurfaceWgsl`, with a CPU mirror) histogram-equalises the noise so the coverage knob means area. Second, SSR on top: each opaque physical surface writes `2 − roughness` into HDR alpha (`surface.ts`; untagged alpha stays 1), so a volume slot can read this frame's scene colour and roughness without a G-buffer. The slot marches a mirror ray at a quarter of the internal size against a nearest-depth copy at that size, with a jittered start, a thickness test, five bisections, screen-edge and distance fades and Ashwood's weight; Ashwood's vertical streak blur and temporal accumulation follow, and bright hits roll off softly instead of clamping, so lamp heads stay bright with no fireflies. A hit replaces the fog sky the scene pass already reflects; a miss leaves that sky alone. Normals are rebuilt from depth at the exact loaded texel, which fixed striped normals in the half/quarter-res volume passes. Two details from Ashwood matter as much as the passes: the asphalt's normal map strength (0.45 → 1.1), and specular AA that keeps the asphalt's lost mip variance (×2, capped 0.05) inside puddles, so far puddles go satin instead of mirroring the horizon as a sharp teal patch. The fog-scatter wet reflection now jitters its four lobe strata per pixel and frame, which removed a bright dot at the end of each lamp streak. **Why it matters:** the booth, gate barrier and poles now mirror in the road with puddle breaks, and road structure against Ashwood went from 0.959–0.978 to 0.969–0.983 (grid metric in `scripts/reflections-crops.py`). **Status:** **have** in the Lot (Night panel › Reflections; Today is one setting away). Games that don't construct `Ssr` are unchanged. **Cost:** see the table. SwiftShader: the Lot's local smoke presented frames at 8.7 s (CI took 30 s before this change). Side-by-sides: `~/code/home/artifacts/lot-2026-10-20/reflections-{title,gate,road}.jpg`.

### Entries — Vegetation

- **Clump-correlated curved 3D grass over clump-correlated turf shader:** Groups blades into golden-angle clusters (`scatterGrassClumps`) sharing lean direction, height, and colour family; shapes each blade (`emitCurvedGrassBlade` in `packages/kit/gpu/foliage.ts:1332`) with parabolic width taper $w(y) = w_0(1 - y^2)$, quadratic tilt + cubic arch, cylindrical cross-section normal rotation ($\pm 55^\circ$), dark root AO (`y^0.65`), and negative `aux` encoding normalized arc coordinate $t \in (0, 1]$ for quadratic wind bend + arc-length height drop in `vsStatic`. Fuses seamlessly into the underlying `evalGrassSoil` + `lawn_grass.webp` turf shader. **Why it matters:** Eliminates flat intersecting "green caltrop" triangles while keeping all grass in the opaque `fsSolid` pipeline with full Apple TBDR Hidden-Surface Removal. **Status:** **have** (`packages/kit/gpu/foliage.ts`, `games/outbreak/src/level.ts:125`).
- **Spherized dome normals + 3-shell canopy AO on pointed V-folded leaf sprigs:** `emitBoughCluster` (`packages/kit/gpu/foliage.ts:834`) builds each bough from core, mid, and sunlit outer shells of pointed V-folded botanical leaf sprigs (`emitLeafSprig`), blending geometric face normals (`fn.y >= 0`) `85–95%` toward the ellipsoidal sub-cloud and crown dome normals (`domeNormalAt`) and encoding sRGB-calibrated interior core shadow (`coreDark >= 0.46` sRGB so `pow(c, 2.2)` stays deep forest green rather than quantizing to black). **Why it matters:** Gives plush, billowy tree and boxwood/hydrangea crowns at 42 m gameplay zoom while retaining crisp individual pointed leaves at 8 m close inspect, with zero `discard` or alpha-card borders. **Status:** **have** (`packages/kit/gpu/foliage.ts:660–918`).
- **Interactive character/zombie foliage contact bending:** `foliageTrample` pushes grass blades (weight `bladeT`) and bush leaves (`aux = 3 + flex`, weight `0.75·flex`) away from up to eight actor ground positions with a `(1 - d/R)²` falloff, drops them by part of the push so blades press toward the soil, and tilts the normal so the parted ring catches light. Radius and strength are the grass look's `trampleRadius`/`trampleStrength` (live uniforms). Outbreak packs the player and the seven nearest living zombies each frame into `OL.vegActors`; the Lab's walkers use the same packing. **Status:** **have**; the Lab's `grass-bend-off`/`grass-bend-on` capture pair is the A/B at an identical tick. **Source:** Lamplight (`docs/game-techniques.md:168`, `paper.wgsl:73`).
- **Leaf mounds under scalloped rosettes:** each bough cluster and bush lobe is a closed, jittered ellipsoid mound (apex fan + two rings, ~54 vertices) shaded deep-to-mid by height, with 2–4 scalloped rosettes (alternating flared tips and tucked clefts around a raised centre) and an optional pointed sprig on its outer shell. The mound removes see-through gaps from the top-down camera; rosettes give the silhouette leaf edges in the opaque no-discard pipeline. Bushes encode `aux = 3 + flex`, which every foliage shader decodes with `foliageTipWeight`/`foliageTranslucency`, so bushes get their own wind, translucency and bending.

- **Forest masses and LOD (`gpu/forest.ts`):** trees are per-variant templates (LOD0 and LOD1 card models, trunk + foliage) drawn instanced from nearest-first lists rebuilt every 2 m of eye movement, plus one camera-facing impostor per far tree whose band is evaluated per vertex (out-of-band instances collapse to a clipped point). Neighbouring levels cross-fade over `fade` metres with complementary Bayer 4×4 masks shifted every frame, so TAA resolves the blend with no holes or double coverage; trunks switch at the band middle. From `thinStart` to `thinEnd` up to `thinFraction` of far crowns drop out by a stable per-tree seed and the survivors grow about their base by `crown/√(1−f)`, so the far canopy keeps (or grows) its coverage and reads as a few dark masses. `cardShadingHooks` restores Ashwood's card shading lost in the first conversion: limb bases darken toward the trunk (`0.45 + 0.55·inner`, inner from the source's `flex` attribute in vertex aux) and the card's own face normal blends 45 % into the crown normal; the same hook darkens the undergrowth clumps that read as pale squares before. **Why it matters:** Kit's note on the Lot vs Ashwood: "his trees are thicker and kind of connect, make a better silhouette". **Proof:** `packages/kit/gpu/forest.test.ts` (complementary fades, thinning keeps coverage, list completeness), `games/lot/scripts/capture-forest.ts` (stills + paired GPU).
- **Rule-based ground clutter (`sim/debris.ts`):** rules name an item, a density group, features to collect along (polylines, box perimeters, circles), a perpendicular offset band, spacing, cluster size and scale; a spatial hash of padded boxes, circles and capsules rejects candidates too close to lanes, doors and colliders. Each rule draws from its own stream and every candidate draws the same numbers at any density, so raising a density only adds items. The Lot uses it for weeds at the fence base and kerbs, leaf litter at kerbs, walls and props, gravel in gutters and shoulders and a little litter; the plant clumps are Ashwood's own undergrowth templates.

### Entries — Atmosphere & Volumetrics

- **Corner-safe wall-clipped volumetric smoke & Blackbody explosion plumes:** `SmokeVolumes` (`packages/kit/fx/volume.ts`) propagates gas across a `48×48` (`0.5 m`) grid using 16-direction weighted moves (< 2.75% angular error), separating solid wall occupancy from `rg16float` density and blocking diagonal filter taps across wall corners. `VolumeRenderer` ray-marches 24 steps at `<= 480×320` against stored 4× MSAA depth and upsamples with 4 depth-aware bilateral taps before bloom. Explosions (`packages/kit/fx/explosion.ts`) pair high-res domain-warped Planckian blackbody fireball billows (`kind = 7`, where outer convex lobes crust into dark charcoal soot while inner fissures glow white-yellow) with a blast-eased volumetric soot plume that shifts from `#13110f` charcoal to warm umber and ash-grey. **Status:** **have** (`packages/kit/fx/volume.ts`, `explosion.ts`). Cross-link: [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu).
- **Analytical ray-cone volumetric flashlight & lamp air (`flashlight.wgsl` / `officeLampAir`):** Solves the ray-cone or ray-sphere quadratic analytically (`disc = along² - dot(delta, delta) + R²`) to bound the ray segment `[start, end]` where the camera ray crosses a flashlight beam or porch lamp cone, integrates Beer–Lambert transmittance differences `weight = max(0, before - after)` with Henyey–Greenstein forward scattering and 3D drifting noise, and gates by the light's shadow visibility (`shadowAt`). **Why it matters:** Gives the survivor's flashlight and porch sconces tangible dusty air cones over dark streets and inside dark rooms without a full-screen froxel pass. **Status:** **have** for Outbreak's flashlight as a separate reduced-resolution pass (`games/outbreak/src/flashlight-beam.ts`); street lamps light their air through the kit fog (`fog-scatter.ts`); the look shader's in-scene version (`makeLookSceneShader`) remains for the Lab vignette. **Source:** Blog (`src/flashlight.wgsl`, `src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanLampLighting.ts:77`, `perf/flashlight.md`).
- **Equiangular lamp scattering in analytic height fog (`@arcade/kit/gpu/fog-scatter`):** Ported from Aiden's Afterhours. The fog is exponential height fog with a closed-form optical depth along the view ray (`fogOpticalDepth`, checked against a numeric march in `fog-scatter.test.ts`). Each frame `FogLampPicker` keeps the 16 frustum-touching lamps whose light in the fog matters most (intensity · σ(lamp) · T(eye → lamp) over distance past range), with 1.5× hysteresis on stable ids so lamps don't swap in and out as their scores cross. Per pixel, each lamp gets 4 equiangular samples (Kulla & Fajardo) along the view ray, clipped to its range. Each sample is a downward cone with a side leak (`leak`, the small halo above the head) times Henyey–Greenstein forward scattering, scaled by local density and the transmittance to the eye. Cone angles come from `SODIUM_CONE`/`fogCone`, the same values the ground pool uses. The sky is the fog seen to `FOG_SKY_DISTANCE`, so the horizon is fully fogged with no seam. It is also the surface ambient, a quadratic in `n.y` fitted on the CPU from a numeric cosine convolution, so there is no env bake. Wet ground traces the reflected ray through the same lamps with vertical jitter, which stretches the reflection into long streaks, plus a narrow per-lamp glint. The pass runs as volume slot N (`FrameInput.volume` array; slot 0 is Outbreak's flashlight) at half resolution with its own temporal history: it reprojects with the unjittered view-projection and resets on cuts, `setLevel`, and eye jumps over 1.5 m. A depth-aware 4-tap upsample then composites it after TAA with no reactive-mask entry, and it writes alpha 0 so Outbreak's shroud still dims it. The jitter is IGN slid 5.588 px a frame; golden-ratio offsets left a diagonal pattern in converged wet reflections. `PostFinishOptions.bloomGate` limits bloom to light sources. `packLampGlow` (sprite kind 9) draws a slimmer lamp head that keeps its energy. Every parameter is a typed Lab setting (`fogControls`), and **Today** is one select away. **Status:** **have** in the kit; in Outbreak it is the Lab option Backquote › Lamp fog, with Today as the default. **Proof:** `scripts/fog-gpu-proof.ts` (Deno, Metal): adds about 0.027 at the lamp head, the cone under the head is about 18× the halo above it, alpha is unchanged, and history cuts flicker about 3.6×. `scripts/fog-scene/scene.ts` is the shared test lot. **Source:** Aiden (`games/afterhours/src/render/post.ts`).
- **Atmosphere over the fog: drifting bank, fog-depth blur, lit cloud deck (`fog-scatter.ts`, `fog-blur.ts`):** For Kit's lot notes ("his fog descends more", "shadier up there, more atmospheric", trees massing into silhouettes). All three are typed rows in `atmosphereControls` (Backquote › Atmosphere), each led by a Today select, and need the fog on. *Bank:* the fog volume marches the ray's segment inside a slab `[base − 1, base + height·(1 + undulation/2)]`, clipped to the surface and a reach (140 m, faded over its last 40 %), in 6 IGN-jittered steps; density is `max(n − gaps, 0)/(1 − gaps) · (1 − h)^1.2` where `n` mixes two 3D value-noise octaves (the second 2.3× finer, counter-drifting and warped by the first) and `h` is height shifted by the noise, so the top rolls. Colour is the fog colour × brightness plus the glow map at the scattering centroid. The slot now composites `scene · T + in-scatter` (`VolumeLightShader.transmittance`, HDR alpha kept), so the bank hides what is behind it; lamp beams keep √T and scatter `1 + σ̄bank/σlamp` more inside it, and wet reflections behind it keep T. *Blur:* after the fog volume, a half-res level of 4 bilinear taps each weighted by its own fog amount `1 − exp(−reach · (τ_height + τ̄_bank))` (premultiplied, so an unfogged trunk never bleeds into the sky behind it), a 13-tap quarter level, then a full-res alpha mix by strength · amount. The bloom pyramid can't be reused because its first level is gated to emitters. *Clouds:* a plane `cloudHeight` above the base, drawn only where it is nearer than the surface (the lot's sky is a box at 699 m), 4 octaves of 2D noise each faded to its mean once a pixel's footprint on the deck (`t · pixelAngle / rd.y`) spans a quarter of its wavelength, edge band `2.2 · footprint`, seen through the height fog to the deck, body `cloudDark × fog` (darker than the sky, so the top goes shadier) plus `cloudGlow ×` the glow map. The glow map is `Σ colour · I / (d_h² + h² + spread²) · 0.1/π` on 32² texels over the lamps' footprint, baked on the CPU when lamps change and stored after the 16 lamps in the scatter lamp buffer (binding 19 is now `array<vec4f>`). `fogScatterVolume({ atmosphere: true })` compiles it in; without it the shader is the plain fog, so Outbreak and the lot's CI smoke are unchanged. **Known limit:** the lot's booth glass writes no depth, so the bank behind the hollow booth draws over its pane. **Cost:** see the table; SwiftShader (pro/studio, 640×360, wall clock over the running loop) was 1.2–2.2 s a frame with every variant inside that noise, and the CI smoke compiles and runs Today. **Proof:** `fog-scatter.test.ts` (Today packing, glow rebake, slab and blur mirrors, naga for the atmosphere and blur shaders), `games/lot/scripts/check-shaders.ts`, stills in `~/code/home/artifacts/lot-2026-10-20/atmosphere-*.jpg`.
- **Shadow-gated 3D drifting dust motes (`dustAt` / `MAT.mote`):** Either evaluates 6–7 exponential depth slices of drifting 2D grid cells in the post/surface shader (`dustAt` in `openPlanPost.ts:487`) or expands camera-facing `MAT.mote` quads in `vsStatic` (`packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts:181`), multiplying each mote's brightness by `shadowAt` / `sunCookie` so motes only glint when drifting through unoccluded light shafts or flashlight beams. **Why it matters:** Adds immediate atmospheric life to dusty house interiors and flashlight cones; gating by `shadowAt` fixes the Blackout audit bug where `MAT.mote` glows through walls (`docs/research/game-notes.md:961`). **Status:** **have** in Outbreak (`games/outbreak/src/air/dust.ts`, per-light lattice, polar-shadowed) and Night Shift (`games/nightshift/src/polish/dust.ts`, spot-map shadowed); **partial** in Blackout (`MAT.mote` in `packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts` lacks `shadowAt`). **Source:** Blog (`src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanPost.ts:487`).

### Entries — Post & Grade

- **Identity-guarded `LookSettings` pipeline (`packages/kit/gpu/look.ts`):** When `isIdentityLook(look)` is true (`preset === "current"`), `Renderer` binds its untouched baseline pipelines for byte-identical output (`diffBytes = 0`). When a non-identity preset or custom slider is active, `Renderer` lazily compiles `makeLookSceneShader` and `makeLookPostShader`, providing 4 tonemappers (ACES, Lamplight Filmic, Sobotka AgX, ACES Hue-Preserving), mid-grey `0.18` contrast pivot, exponential shoulder, shadow toe, pre-split-tone warm practical hue retention (`warmBias`), film halation, 3-band smoothstepped diffuse wrap, and a 2-ring 4-sector brushstroke-warped Kuwahara painter's filter. **Status:** **have** (`packages/kit/gpu/look.ts`, `packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts:989`). Cross-link: [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu).
- **Temporal AA and upscaling (`packages/kit/gpu/temporal.ts`, AR-72):** `Renderer.setTemporal({ mode, renderScale, sharpen, history })` renders the scene at `renderScale` per axis (`"taau"`) or native (`"taa"`) with a Halton(2,3) sub-pixel jitter (8 phases native, 18 at 0.67, 32 at 0.5) and resolves into a canvas-size history: 3×3 Blackman-Harris reconstruction (a tight output-pixel kernel to accumulate, a wide input-pixel kernel where there is no history), reprojection by depth from the closest of the 3×3 taps, rejection where the previous frame's linear depth disagrees by more than 1.2% (disocclusion) or the history is off screen, a YCoCg variance clip (γ 1.0 still → 0.75 moving; 1.25+ left a torch-pool smear), Catmull-Rom history sampling, a blend in a reversible max-channel tone curve, and RCAS on the way out (history stays unsharpened). 4× MSAA stays on in the (smaller) scene pass: the sample count is baked into every pipeline, game plugins included, and MSAA keeps edges on moving actors whose history is rejected. The camera is the only motion the resolve knows (the velocity buffer exists only in the shader-less motion-blur mode). History drops on an eye jump over 4 m, resize, mode change, `setLevel` and `resetTemporalHistory()` (Outbreak calls it on Restart, New town and regenerate, where the camera may not move). Off-browser proof `deno run -A --sloppy-imports scripts/temporal-gpu-proof.ts` (real pipelines, analytic scene): frame-to-frame shimmer 0.167 → 0.026, a 5 px/frame box and a sweeping torch pool leave no trail, a popping box shows on its first frame. **Rules for passes in the scene (fire, smoke, beam dust, foliage, weather, reflections):** (1) take the camera from the frame binding (`F.viewProj`, `F.screen.xy`): under TAA they are the jittered matrix and the internal size for that frame; a layer with its own copy of the game's camera must take the renderer's instead (`WeatherRenderer.prepare(input, camera)` gets the jittered matrix and internal size from the renderer; its density and LOD still follow the game's canvas width), or it renders unjittered and aliased; (2) anything that moves on its own goes in the reactive mask (`@arcade/kit/gpu/reactive`), one extra pass after the scene at the internal size (rgba8, one sample, no depth, group 0 the Frame group): it holds the renderer's alpha and additive sprites, `ParticleRenderer` (kit fire, smoke, sparks, debris, breach dust), `WeatherRenderer` (streaks, splashes, drips, mist), `SplatterRenderer`'s drops in flight (not its decals), tyre smoke, `Batch`es flagged `reactive` (Outbreak: breach debris, thrown items, dynamite in flight; not actors or ragdolls, which keep MSAA edges and the clip) and `ScenePass` plugins that implement `drawReactive` (Outbreak's beam dust). A new layer adds a twin pipeline with `reactivePipelineDescriptor(itsDescriptor)` (same stages, its own fragment output is the mask value) and a `drawReactive(pass)`; opaque geometry uses `REACTIVE_COVERAGE_WGSL`. Unmasked, the clip still bounds trails but damps intended flicker. `deno run -A --sloppy-imports scripts/temporal-reactive-proof.ts` reads the mask back per source on real wgpu; (3) no per-frame stochastic noise or dither in the scene pass for a look: the resolve averages it to its mean; grain, dither and shroud noise belong in the composite, which runs after the resolve at canvas size; (4) the HDR alpha passes through unclamped and is clipped to the local 3×3 range (Outbreak packs `1 + 4 × unseen` and the flash mask there; an early `[0, 1]` clamp erased the shroud); (5) the volumetric light (`shaders.volume`) is added after the resolve and never enters the history; (6) screen-space reflections or a mirror that read the HDR should read the current frame, not the history. **Status:** **partial** (Outbreak opt-in, default MSAA until a post-fix look and ghosting run). Cross-link: [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu).
- **Midtone-masked film grain + dark-gated 8-bit IGN dither (`openPlanPost.ts`):** Evaluates triangular dual-hash grain masked by `smoothstep(0.02, 0.2, outLuma) * (1.0 - smoothstep(0.75, 1.0, outLuma))` (boosted inside light shafts by `+ 0.018 * beamLift`) and adds 1-code-value Interleaved Gradient Noise dither `(ign(fragCoord.xy) - 0.5) / 255.0 * lit` before 8-bit output, gated by `lit = smoothstep(0.0, 3.0 / 255.0, max3(rgb))` so pitch-black regions remain noiseless while dark twilight gradients never Mach-band. **Why it matters:** Outbreak's default `postShader` (`packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts:946`) currently lacks 8-bit output dither (`docs/research/game-notes.md:960`) and applies grain at `l = 0`. **Status:** **partial** (`look.ts:1238` has midtone grain; `postShader` in `shaders.ts:945` lacks both midtone mask and IGN dither). **Source:** Blog (`src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanPost.ts:686–702`), [Playdead GDC 2016 *Banding in Games*](https://www.loopit.dk/banding_in_games.pdf).

### Entries — Performance on Apple TBDR / WebGPU

- **Strict `fsSolid` vs `fs` (`discard`) pipeline split & Z-banded wall chunks:** Any pipeline whose fragment shader contains `discard` (or `alphaToCoverageEnabled`) disables Apple Silicon's hardware Hidden-Surface Removal (HSR / early-Z) for every triangle drawn with that pipeline. Keep floors, furniture, exterior trees, grass, instanced characters, and glass in `fsSolid` (zero `discard`), bucket cuttable walls into 4 m Z-bands (`screenChunks` in `games/outbreak/src/level.ts:507`), and route roof chunks to `levelCut` only while the player is inside that building's footprint (`customCutaway`). Cross-link: [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu).
- **Register-pressure & SIMD-occupancy hygiene (`postLightMaterial` + constant array layers):** On Apple GPUs, keeping texture coordinates and sampled material structs live across a 36-register `lightSum` loop halves SIMD occupancy, and indexing a `texture_2d_array` with a branch-divergent variable serializes texture fetches. Evaluate `lightSum` first using base geometry normals, then run `postLightMaterial` (`surfaceMaterial`) with compile-time constant layer indices, and replace small per-pixel `for`/`switch` loops (`interior(p)`) with branchless `select()`. Cross-link: [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu).
- **Single-in-flight pacing & `MessageChannel` timer un-nesting:** Cap `Renderer.inflight` to 1 frame, advance fixed 60 Hz simulation ticks through `FrameClock`, and dispatch sub-frame wakeups via `MessageChannel` (`games/outbreak/src/main.ts:981`) so fast GPU completions (`< 16.67 ms`) never hit Blink's `nesting_level >= 5` 4 ms `setTimeout` clamp or macOS `kqueue` timer slack. Cross-link: [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu), `perf/outbreak-performance.md`.

## 9. Candidates Not Yet Built

From the October 2026 web survey, the Ashwood port and the woods and storm research (full notes, licences and
thumbnails: `~/code/home/artifacts/agent-reports/gfx-research.md`, `engine-comparison.md`,

What the survey proposed and has since shipped is in the tables above: the mist bank, fog-depth blur, lamp-glow map
and cloud deck, thin glass and interior mapping, contact shadows, forest thinning with Bayer cross-fades, rule-based
debris, plan-space bounce (a cone gather rather than radiance cascades), capsule shadows, SSR and the wet term. Costs
below are estimates. Licence rule: MIT/CC0/Unlicense code may be ported with credit; CC BY-NC-SA Shadertoys and
unlicensed repos are technique only.

- **LEAN slope-covariance specular AA on wet ground (web-ocean, MIT):** mip slope moments plus the screen derivatives
  of the slope turn glints into an anisotropic Gaussian that widens to a sheen where a pixel can't resolve them.
  Outbreak's puddle glints use the glint-image version (`glintAA`); the kit's wet surface and the Lot's lamp-lit
  asphalt shimmer (AR-122) don't yet. ~0.05 ms.
- **Dynamic resolution governor (Tidewater, Neon Zenith):** scale the TAAU input resolution from frame time. Enabler.
- **Lens droplets / condensation on the camera (Tidewater `LensDroplets.js`, MIT):** for walking out of rain or fog;
  optional, only where the camera fiction supports it. ~0.02 ms.
- **Stable cutout foliage (woods research):** coverage-preserving alpha mips with alpha-to-coverage, and alpha-aware
  spot-shadow casters, so ferns neither sparkle nor cast card-shaped (or no) shadows.
- **Canopy-openness sky occlusion (woods research):** multiply the sky ambient by a baked openness field so
  clearings, canopy undersides and root pockets separate.
- **First-person torch hit bounce (woods research):** a diffuse emitter just off the beam's nearest hit, 0.5–3 m
  reach, 2–8 % of the direct light; plan bounce doesn't model raised torches or canopies.

### Not worth it now
Gaussian splats (no dynamic night lighting), ReSTIR (the lamp grid already bounds cost), a full froxel pipeline
(only for many local media volumes; revisit for Outbreak smoke over fog), Hillaire LUT atmosphere (nothing to gain
in a fogged night sky), HRC volumetrics (11–12 ms on an RTX 3080), full-scene SDF raymarching, cluster LOD, FFT
ocean, 3D grid fluid fire (~1 ms+ per 64³). Where SDF and raymarching do pay for us: plan-space 2D SDFs for
radiance cascades, short history-accumulated mist marches, derivative-AA analytic edges for road paint, decals
and cloud edges, and closed-form capsule shadows.

### What the good AI-built engines have in common
Tidewater, web-ocean and deepsea (Opus 5.5) each run on a small custom WebGPU engine, implement a long list of
published techniques, and check every change with headless screenshots reviewed by critic agents. They are ahead
of our kit on sky/atmosphere, glass and LOD polish, not architecture.
