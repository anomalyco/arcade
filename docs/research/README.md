# Research

The evidence behind the practical docs: measurements, proofs, studies, parked experiments and sources. Read these
when you need to know why a rule exists or what a technique measured. To decide what to build and how, start from the
practical guides in [docs/](../README.md), beginning with [quality-checklist.md](../quality-checklist.md) for anything
visual.

- [game-notes.md](game-notes.md): the measured-lessons log for every area (proving changes, rendering, art
  direction, crowds, multiplayer, input, feel, audio, researched but not adopted). Add an entry once a technique is
  proven in a game: what it does, where it lives, what it measured. Animation lessons go in
  [animation.md](../animation.md).
- [realism-log.md](realism-log.md): proofs, detailed measurements, candidates awaiting Kit and sources behind
  [realism.md](../realism.md).
- [rendering-techniques.md](rendering-techniques.md): the rendering catalogue (have / partial / missing), how each
  technique works, its cost and where it came from (Arcade, The Open Plan, Lamplight).
- [outbreak-look.md](outbreak-look.md), [outbreak-look-v2.md](outbreak-look-v2.md): Outbreak's first look studies
  against a paint-over (the first was rejected as flat and washed out). The shipped rules are
  [outbreak-art-direction.md](../outbreak-art-direction.md).
- [style-playground.md](style-playground.md): nine presentation styles on Outbreak's room sim, their GPU costs and
  full-game scaling verdicts.
- [`perf/`](../../perf/): raw ledgers and JSON from paired measurements, linked from the entries above.

A practical doc keeps the rule, Kit's decision and one headline number; the measurement that justified it goes here
and is linked.
