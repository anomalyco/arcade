# Rendering Techniques

Catalogue of real-time rendering techniques for Arcade's top-down and three-quarter WebGPU games (`@arcade/kit`, Outbreak, Blackout, Getaway, Carrier), synthesised from:

- **Arcade** (`packages/kit/gpu/`, `packages/kit/fx/`, `packages/kit/textures/`, `docs/game-notes.md`),
- **Kit's OpenCode 2.0 blog game ("The Open Plan")** (`src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlan*.ts`, `src/flashlight.wgsl`), and
- **Lamplight** (`docs/rendering-tricks.md`, `docs/game-techniques.md`, `docs/background-lighting.md`, `docs/hardware-target.md`, `docs/smoke-and-shutter.md`).

Proven per-pass measurements and benchmark logs live in [`docs/game-notes.md`](game-notes.md) and `perf/*.md`; this document catalogues the techniques, their current status in Arcade, and how to apply them.

- **Status key:**
  - **have** — Shipped in `@arcade/kit` or one of the workspace games (file path cited).
  - **partial** — Shipped in one game or Lab section, or simplified compared to the full technique.
  - **missing** — Not yet implemented in Arcade.
- **Cost convention:** Measured or estimated GPU time on Apple Silicon (M4/M5 Max Metal) at `1000×680`–`3456×1894` unless noted otherwise.

### Entries — Lighting & GI

- **Windowed inverse-square + cone practical lights:** Evaluates diffuse and specular in a single loop over culled lights (`lightSum` in `packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts:352`), rejecting on squared plan distance `dPlan2 >= r * r` before `sqrt`, clamping near-fixture distance `dn = max(d3, max(hl, 1.25))` so wall tops and desks directly under a bulb never blow out hotter than the floor, and shaping spotlights with a smooth cubic cone feather. **Why it matters:** Keeps top-down rooms readable without hot-spotting wall caps near the camera. **Status:** **have** (`packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts:352`, `games/getaway/src/render/shaders.ts:83`).
- **CPU + frustum + conservative-bounds light culling:** Culls map lights against the camera ground frustum (`cam.groundRect` + clip planes `inLightFrustum`), partitions shadow-casting lights into a contiguous prefix (`shadowLightCount`), and bounds shadowless suffixes (e.g. Blackout's desktop monitors) with a plan AABB (`F.monitorBounds`) so pixels outside the box skip the suffix entirely. **Why it matters:** Top-down maps have 30–100+ practical lights across a town or office; culling off-screen lamps cut Outbreak interior lights from 28 to 11 (`-3.39 ms` GPU). **Status:** **have** (`games/blackout/src/render/lighting.ts:82`, `games/outbreak/src/main.ts:739`). Cross-link: [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu).
- **Spatial 2D light-list grid (`uLampGrid`):** Precomputes a 2D plan texture (`0.5 m` cells, `RGBA8`) on the CPU where each texel stores the 1-based indices of up to 4 static lamps whose radius overlaps that cell (`openPlanLampLighting.ts:254`). **Why it matters:** Eliminates the per-pixel loop over all 15–32 frustum-visible lights in `lightSum`; each fragment fetches its cell's 4 candidates and exits on the first zero slot. **Status:** **missing** in Arcade. **Cost:** `147 KiB` for a `96×96 m` town at `0.5 m` cells; `< 1 ms` CPU build at level load; saves `~1–2 ms` fragment ALU. **Source:** Blog (`src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanLampLighting.ts:50`).
- **Baked 2D indirect bounce flood-fill (`bounceTex`):** Flood-fills each static light's colour through open floor cells (`0.5 m` grid) using an 8-neighbour corner-safe Dijkstra search blocked by walls $\ge 1.8\text{ m}$ (`bakeBounce` in `games/blackout/src/render/level-mesh.ts:616`). Stores $\sqrt{\text{rgb}}$ in `.rgb` for dark-end precision and wall-free coverage in `.a` (`bounceAt` renormalises by `max(b.a, 0.25)` at `world.xz + n.xz * 0.3`). Linear irradiance arrays are retained on the CPU so intensity/day-night sliders recombine and upload without re-running Dijkstra. **Why it matters:** Lets lit rooms bleed warm colour through open doorways and around corridor corners instead of dropping to uniform grey ambient outside the direct cone. **Status:** **partial** — **have** in Blackout (`games/blackout/src/render/level-mesh.ts:616`, `packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts:445`); **missing** in Outbreak (`games/outbreak/src/level.ts:578` uploads a `1×1` zero texture and `games/outbreak/src/shaders.ts:18` sets `bounce: "vec3f(0.0)"`). Cross-link: [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#art-direction).
- **Baked 2D floor contact AO (`floorTex.r`) & Multi-bounce AO (Jimenez):** Rasterises wall, window, tree, and furniture footprints into a 2D plan grid (`12.5 cm` in Blackout with two 7-tap box blurs; `25 cm` analytic exponential distance in Outbreak), sampled in `shadeScene` with vertical attenuation `low = 1.0 - smoothstep(0.0, 0.9, world.y)` and Jimenez's cubic multi-bounce polynomial so coloured carpets, wood, and grass retain saturated interreflection in crevices rather than turning muddy grey. **Why it matters:** Grounds every wall base, table, sofa, and tree trunk in top-down view with zero per-frame SSAO pass cost. **Status:** **have** (`games/blackout/src/render/level-mesh.ts:497`, `games/outbreak/src/level.ts:529`, `packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts:665`).
- **Baked 3D vertex AO & bevel tone (`aOpenTone`):** Encodes per-vertex contact darkening at object bases (`bottomAo = 0.45..0.55`), bright edge highlights on upper chamfers (`topBoost = 1.18..1.22`), and soft Gaussian floor footprints under props directly into a vertex attribute during procedural mesh construction (`openPlanModels.ts:59–196, 1407`). **Why it matters:** 2D plan-space `floorTex.r` cannot distinguish the underside of a table top, the inside of a sink basin, a recessed window jamb, or an upper cabinet bevel from the floor below it; vertex tone gives crisp 3D Playdead-style form on every prop for zero runtime cost. **Status:** **partial** — `packages/kit/gpu/foliage.ts` bakes root/canopy AO into `v.color.rgb`, but `Mesh.box`/`bevelBox` (`packages/kit/gpu/mesh.ts`) leave `v.color.a` unused (`1.0`). **Source:** Blog (`src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanModels.ts`).
- **Analytical rectangular area-light floor bounce (`officeFloorBounce`):** Given the room-space rectangle `uLitFloor = vec4(x0, x1, z0, z1)` where sunlight or window light lands on the floor, clamps any surface point's `xz` to `uLitFloor` at floor height to find the nearest lit floor point and evaluates `lowRoom * (facing * 0.85 + 0.15) / (1.0 + d2 * 0.6)` (`openPlanAtmosphere.ts:22`). **Why it matters:** Gives instant, noise-free 1-bounce floor-to-wall and floor-to-character illumination around window shafts and porch pools in 6 ALU ops. **Status:** **missing** in Arcade. **Source:** Blog (`src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanAtmosphere.ts:22`), Lamplight (`docs/rendering-tricks.md` §1.10).
- **Analytical clipped rectangular area light & rough specular sheen (`openGlassLight` / `openGlassSheen`):** Integrates the exact Lambertian form factor of a rectangular emitter (`openGlassEdge` rational approximation) clipped against its reveal/sill horizon (`hwGlassFloor`), plus a solid-angle-weighted rough specular lobe (`openGlassSheen`) and soft planar window reflection (`hwDeskWindow`, blurring `clerestoryOpen` by `0.035 + t * rough² * 0.16`). **Why it matters:** Replaces circular point-light hotspots at windows and TV/monitor screens with true rectangular light throws and stretched window reflections across floors and wet driveways. **Status:** **missing** in Arcade (Outbreak approximates window pools with a `rx²·ry²` point-light mask in `games/outbreak/src/shaders.ts:21`). **Source:** Blog (`src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanHardware.ts:113–284`), [Heitz et al. 2016](https://eheitzresearch.wordpress.com/415-2/).
- **Analytical window cookie + canopy dapple (`sunCookie` / `clerestoryCanopy`):** Projects world points along the sun/moon vector to the window plane or overhead tree canopy, box-filtering panes and mullions over a kernel width `w = w0 + throwDist * spread` (sharp near the sill, soft across the room) and multiplying by wind-advected multi-scale canopy/cloud waves. **Why it matters:** Makes outdoor streets, lawns, and indoor window patches feel alive with moving leaf dapple and contact-hardening mullion shadows without high-res shadow cascades. **Status:** **partial** — Blackout bakes a static `3.125 cm` R8 window mask (`games/blackout/src/render/level-mesh.ts:547`); Outbreak stamps static `3 m` interior window boxes (`games/outbreak/src/level.ts:544`). **Source:** Blog (`src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanSun.ts:57`), Lamplight (`docs/rendering-tricks.md` §1.6), [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu).
- **L1 Spherical Harmonics / azimuthal sky ambient:** Projects the time-of-day sky dome (cool zenith, warm solar-azimuth horizon, ground bounce) into L1/L2 Spherical Harmonics (or 3D probe grid `BakedLighting` in Lamplight) so shadowed normals facing the sunset/moon catch warm/cool directional fill while downward normals catch ground bounce. **Why it matters:** Prevents flat, uniform ambient on vertical walls and character silhouettes when out of direct light. **Status:** **missing** (`ambientAt(n)` in `packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts:452` is 1D vertical `n.y` only). **Source:** Lamplight (`docs/rendering-tricks.md` §1.7, `docs/background-lighting.md:83`).

### Entries — Shadows

- **GPU polar 1D ray-cast shadow map (`polarShader`):** Casts `POLAR_RAYS = 512` rays per culled light in a compute pass (`workgroup_size(64)`) against axis-aligned wall/furniture slabs and circular person casters. Stores the nearest full-height blocker (`full`) plus the two nearest partial-height blockers `(t0, t1, h)` per ray (`polarOut`), so low tables, counters, sofas, and window sills cast finite-length shadows (`partialShadow`) while walls cast infinite shadows. **Why it matters:** Gives omnidirectional point-light and cone-light shadows for up to 64 lights in a single compute pass without 64 cubemap render passes. **Status:** **have** (`packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts:63–343`). Cross-link: [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu).
- **Interpolated multi-tap polar visibility PCF:** Evaluates `tapVis` at adjacent integer rays `(lo, lo + 1)` and interpolates *visibility* (never blocker distance, which leaks across silhouettes) across a 5-tap (Outbreak) or 9-tap world-hashed (Blackout) angular kernel, reusing `prevVis` when `lo == prevLo`. **Why it matters:** Eliminates radial staircase bands on floors and tables without a fullscreen blur pass. **Status:** **have** (`packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts:293–336`). Cross-link: [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu).
- **Static CPU-rasterised radial depth atlas for local lamps (`openPlanLampLighting.ts`):** Rasterises opaque scene triangles once on the CPU (or in a Web Worker: `openPlanSceneWorker.ts`) into a `512×512` R32F radial-depth atlas (`16` tiles of `128×128`), sampled with 2×2 PCF (`officeLampVisibility`) for both surface pools and volumetric lamp air. **Why it matters:** Static room/porch/desk lamps never move; pre-rasterising their blocker depth once per seed eliminates per-frame shadow-caster gathering and compute ray-casting for static fixtures. **Status:** **missing** in Arcade. **Cost:** `+0.075 ms` paired frame delta on M2 Max (`perf/open-plan-lamps.md`); `1.01 MiB` texture. **Source:** Blog (`src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanLampLighting.ts:136`).
- **Texel-snapped orthographic directional shadow map + dirty-gated static cache:** Renders directional sun/moon casters into a `2048²` or `4096×H` depth texture with projection origin snapped to texel increments (`round(origin * res) / res`), culling chunks that neither intersect the camera frustum nor cast a shadow sweeping into the camera frustum (`aabbInFrustum` with `sweep` in `games/getaway/src/render/renderer.ts:551`). When the camera or sun is stationary (or static geometry is cached into `static_shadow_tex` as in Lamplight), static casters are not redrawn. **Why it matters:** Outbreak currently has **no directional sun/moon shadow map** (`docs/outbreak-look-v2.md:91`), so houses, fences, and trees cast no directional exterior shadows across the street or lawn. **Status:** **partial** — **have** in Getaway (`games/getaway/src/render/renderer.ts:534`); **missing** in Outbreak and Blackout. **Source:** [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu), Blog (`openPlanSun.ts:171`), Lamplight (`docs/rendering-tricks.md` §1.1).
- **Analytical capsule & foot contact shadows:** Evaluates soft cone/ellipsoid occlusion from character feet and bodies against the ground plane in the surface shader (or draws height-faded ground decals like Getaway's `kind == 9` wheel-weighted car shadow in `games/getaway/src/render/shaders.ts:489` and the blog game's `hwDiskSkyShadow` / `dressingContact`). **Why it matters:** Grounds survivors, zombies, and small props in overcast or ambient-lit interiors where polar point lights are dim or absent. **Status:** **partial** — Getaway has car/tree/building grounding decals (`games/getaway/src/render/shaders.ts:489`); Outbreak and Blackout rely on round polar casters (`m.y > 0.5`) and lack indirect foot-contact shadows. **Source:** Lamplight (`docs/rendering-tricks.md` §1.5, `docs/ruins-lighting-review.md`), Blog (`openPlanHardware.ts:307`).

### Entries — Materials & Textures

- **Footprint-filtered (`fwidth`) procedural material recipes & Specular AA:** Computes world-space pixel footprint `surfaceFootprint = max(length(dpdx(o.world)), length(dpdy(o.world)))` in uniform control flow at fragment entry (`packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts:695`), band-limits procedural seams (`seam`) and grain (`grain`) toward their analytic mean before Nyquist aliasing, and folds unresolved micro-normal variance into roughness via `roughnessToGloss` (`effRough = sqrt(rough² + normalVariance * clamp(surfaceFootprint * 12, 0, 1))`, with `effRough >= 0.72` clamping broad specular to zero). **Why it matters:** Prevents sub-pixel shingle/plank/cobble shimmer during camera pan and avoids WGSL non-uniform derivative errors on Metal. **Status:** **have** (`packages/kit/gpu/surface-materials.ts:536–955`). Cross-link: [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu).
- **Generated flat-diffuse WebP texture array + linear-light mips + procedural breakup:** Prepares 16 `256×256` RGBA WebP textures (`1,098 KB` total) from unlit orthographic `gpt-image-1.5` candidates using mirrored-quadrant wrapping for isotropic surfaces, toroidal raised-cosine blending for horizontal courses (wood planks, brick, clapboard, shingles), and bilateral symmetry for area rugs, with a 9-level CPU linear-light mip chain (`buildLinearMipChain`) and authored roughness in alpha. In WGSL, `sampleMaterialFast` uses **compile-time constant array layer indices** per material branch and runs in `postLightMaterial` *after* `lightSum`. **Why it matters:** Dynamic `texture_2d_array` layer indexing across divergent branches stalled Apple Silicon SIMD groups (`41.8 ms → 24.2 ms`); moving material/texture evaluation after `lightSum` so texture locals don't overlap the 36-register shadow loop cut Outbreak interior GPU time to `14.47 ms`. **Status:** **have** (`packages/kit/textures/`, `packages/kit/gpu/surface-materials.ts`). Cross-link: [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu).
- **Local-space triplanar wear projection:** Projects a small unlit wear/patina texture in object-local coordinates using tight power-4 normal weights `w = pow(abs(n), vec3(4.0)) / dot(pow(abs(n), vec3(4.0)), vec3(1.0))` to modulate albedo, $F_0$, and roughness together (`openPlanHardware.ts:400`, `openPlanHardwareFinishes.ts:25`, Lamplight `docs/hardware-target.md`). **Why it matters:** Outbreak's interior furniture (`sofa`, `bed`, `table`, `fridge`, `stove`, `counter`) and parked car still use flat vertex colours; triplanar wear modulation gives 3D props tactile edge rubs, enamel chips, and fabric grain without UV unwrapping. **Status:** **missing** on props/vehicles in Arcade. **Source:** Blog (`openPlanHardware.ts:395`), Lamplight (`docs/hardware-target.md`).

### Entries — Performance on Apple TBDR / WebGPU

- **Strict `fsSolid` vs `fs` (`discard`) pipeline split & Z-banded wall chunks:** Any pipeline whose fragment shader contains `discard` (or `alphaToCoverageEnabled`) disables Apple Silicon's hardware Hidden-Surface Removal (HSR / early-Z) for every triangle drawn with that pipeline. Keep floors, furniture, exterior trees, grass, instanced characters, and glass in `fsSolid` (zero `discard`), bucket cuttable walls into 4 m Z-bands (`screenChunks` in `games/outbreak/src/level.ts:507`), and route roof chunks to `levelCut` only while the player is inside that building's footprint (`customCutaway`). Cross-link: [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu).
- **Register-pressure & SIMD-occupancy hygiene (`postLightMaterial` + constant array layers):** On Apple GPUs, keeping texture coordinates and sampled material structs live across a 36-register `lightSum` loop halves SIMD occupancy, and indexing a `texture_2d_array` with a branch-divergent variable serializes texture fetches. Evaluate `lightSum` first using base geometry normals, then run `postLightMaterial` (`surfaceMaterial`) with compile-time constant layer indices, and replace small per-pixel `for`/`switch` loops (`interior(p)`) with branchless `select()`. Cross-link: [game-notes.md](game-notes.md#rendering-webgpu).
- **Single-in-flight pacing & `MessageChannel` timer un-nesting:** Cap `Renderer.inflight` to 1 frame, advance fixed 60 Hz simulation ticks through `FrameClock`, and dispatch sub-frame wakeups via `MessageChannel` (`games/outbreak/src/main.ts:981`) so fast GPU completions (`< 16.67 ms`) never hit Blink's `nesting_level >= 5` 4 ms `setTimeout` clamp or macOS `kqueue` timer slack. ### Why Baking Direct Practical Light Alone Cannot Replace the Dynamic Flashlight
- **3D normal & specular response:** A 2D floor lightmap only records irradiance for $n = (0, 1, 0)$. Outbreak's interior furniture, door panels, window mullions, and wet asphalt/porch walkways rely on 3D incident light vectors `ldir` for normal shading (`dot(n, ldir)`) and grazing specular highlights (`lit.spec`).
- **Moving shadows:** The player's flashlight (`lights[1]`) and muzzle flashes (`lights[2]`) move every tick at 60 Hz and cast sweeping polar shadows off walls, doors, and furniture (`shadowAt`).
- **The winning split:** Keep **direct practical light + dynamic flashlight** live in `lightSum` (accelerated by `uLampGrid` / cached static polar rows), and bake **indirect bounce (`bounceTex`) + fine directional moon/canopy occlusion (`moonTex`) + 3D vertex AO/bevel tone (`v.color.a`)**.

### Recommended Concrete First Step for `outbreak-lighting`
1. **Wire real `bounceTex` in `games/outbreak/src/level.ts` (`compileLevel`):**
   - Port/share Blackout's 8-neighbour corner-safe Dijkstra flood-fill (`bakeBounce` in `games/blackout/src/render/level-mesh.ts:616`) into `@arcade/kit/gpu/level.ts` and run it at `192×192` (`0.5 m` cells, `~4 ms` CPU) or `384×384` (`0.25 m` cells) over `town.lamps` (`room`, `porch`, `window`, `street`).
   - Store $\sqrt{\text{rgb}}$ in `bounceTex.rgb` and non-wall coverage in `bounceTex.a`.
   - In `games/outbreak/src/shaders.ts`, replace `bounce: "vec3f(0.0)"` with `bounceAt(o.world.xz + n.xz * 0.3) * (0.36 * nightLamps)` so warm indirect bounce fills rooms, hallways, and porches and automatically scales with `dayCycle(hour)` without rebaking!
2. **Bake exterior building & tree-crown directional moon/twilight shadows into `floor.moon` (`moonTex` at `@binding(8)`):**
   - In `compileLevel()`, allocate `moon = new Uint8Array(768 * 768)` (`12.5 cm/texel`, `576 KiB`) and march rays along the dominant dusk/night moon direction against `town.buildings` (walls + pitched roof ridges `ridge = 4.35 m`), `town.barriers`, and `town.props` (`tree` crowns as soft spherical/dappled attenuators), plus 2×2 sub-texel window mullion coverage inside houses (matching Blackout's `perf/blackout-window-light.md`).
3. **Pack 3D vertex AO / bevel tone into `v.color.a` (`packages/kit/gpu/mesh.ts` & `shaders.ts`):**
   - Use the spare 4th byte of `v.color` (`unorm8x4` at offset 16) to store `tone` (`0.5 = 1.0×` neutral, `< 0.5` darkens furniture/wall bottoms and soffits, `> 0.5` boosts upper chamfers/bevels).

## 10. Top 10 "Steal from the Blog Game" Tips for Outbreak (Ranked by Visual Impact)

Each tip is ranked by expected visual impact on Outbreak's top-down look, citing its exact implementation in Kit's OpenCode 2.0 blog game (`~/code/experiments/blog-notes-snapshot/`) and where it maps into Arcade's renderer.

### 1. Analytical Window & Porch Light Cookie with Distance-Widening Penumbra & Canopy Sway
- **Blog game source:** `src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanSun.ts` (`sunCookieGlsl`, `clerestoryOpen`, `clerestoryCanopy`, lines 57–141).
- **How it works:** Projects any room/yard point back along the light vector to the window plane (`q = room - SUN_ROOM * t`), analytically box-filters the window panes, sash frames, and mullions over a kernel width `wSun = 0.012 + t * spread * uSunSoft` that widens with distance from the glass (sharp near the sill, soft across the room), mixes in a wider sky aperture `wSky` so wall piers don't leave hard black stripes, and modulates by multi-scale drifting tree-branch and cloud waves (`clerestoryCanopy`).
- **Map to Outbreak:** In `games/outbreak/src/shaders.ts` (`directional` and `surface` hooks), evaluate an analytical window/porch aperture + drifting 2-octave tree-canopy cookie (`clerestoryCanopy`) on both interior window shafts and exterior moonlit lawns/roofs.

### 2. Analytical Ray-Cone Volumetric Beams + Shadow-Gated 3D Drifting Dust Motes
- **Blog game source:** `src/flashlight.wgsl`, `src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanLampLighting.ts` (`officeLampAir`, lines 75–104), and `src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanPost.ts` (`dustAt`, lines 487–535).
- **How it works:** Solves the ray-cone / ray-sphere quadratic analytically to find `[t0, t1]` where the view ray crosses a flashlight or desk-lamp cone, integrates Beer–Lambert transmittance differences `max(0, T(t0) - T(t1))` with Henyey–Greenstein forward scattering and 3D drifting value noise, and layers 6–7 depth slices of drifting 3D dust motes (`dustAt`) that only glint where `cookie * shadowTap > 0.01`.
- **Map to Outbreak:** In `games/outbreak/src/shaders.ts` (`surface` hook), evaluate analytical ray-cone volumetric scatter + drifting dust motes for the survivor's flashlight (`lights[1]`, gated by `shadowAt(1u, ...)`) and porch lamps (`lamp.kind === "porch"`), giving the flashlight a physical dusty beam in default gameplay (`Current` look as well as `Noir`).

### 3. Analytical Rectangular Area-Light Floor Bounce (`officeFloorBounce`)
- **Blog game source:** `src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanAtmosphere.ts` (`officeFloorBounce`, lines 22–29).
- **How it works:** Treats each lit floor patch (`uLitFloor = vec4(x0, x1, z0, z1)`) as a rectangular area source on the floor: any nearby wall, furniture side, or character clamps its XZ to `uLitFloor` at floor height (`nearest`), computes `to = nearest - room`, and adds `lowRoom * (facing * 0.85 + 0.15) / (1.0 + d2 * 0.6) * mix(1.0, ao, 0.5)`.
- **Map to Outbreak:** In `games/outbreak/src/shaders.ts`, bounce warm light from lit room floor pools and porch walkways onto vertical interior walls (`abs(n.y) < 0.5`), kitchen cabinet fronts, and survivor/zombie legs (`mat == 12`) in 6 ALU ops.

### 4. Baked Per-Vertex Contact AO, Bevel Boost & Under-Prop Footprints (`aOpenTone`)
- **Blog game source:** `src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanModels.ts` (`MeshBuilder.box`, `MeshBuilder.bevelBox`, `buildForegroundDesk`, `buildFloor`, lines 149–196, 540–560, 1407–1428).
- **How it works:** Every box and chamfered bevel assigns `bottomAo = 0.45..0.55` at its base, `topBoost = 1.18` on upper faces, and `tRim = topBoost * 1.22` on front-top chamfers, while `buildFloor` bakes soft rectangular `Footprint` shadows under every desk, cabinet, chair, and bucket.
- **Map to Outbreak:** Store `tone` in the unused alpha byte `v.color.a` of `Mesh` (`packages/kit/gpu/mesh.ts`, offset 19 of `VERT_BYTES = 24`) and multiply `o.color` or `ao` in `packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts` (`vsStatic` / `shadeScene`). Instant 3D self-occlusion on every bed, sofa, table, counter, door, and house trim with 0 extra vertex bytes.

### 5. Analytical Clipped Rectangular Area Light & Stretched Specular Sheen for Windows
- **Blog game source:** `src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanHardware.ts` (`openGlassLight`, `openGlassSheen`, `hwDeskWindow`, lines 113–158, 273–284).
- **How it works:** Integrates the solid-angle form factor of a rectangular window/CRT emitter clipped against its frame/sill horizon (`openGlassLight`), plus an elongated rough specular lobe (`openGlassSheen`) and distance/roughness-blurred window reflection (`hwDeskWindow`).
- **Map to Outbreak:** Replace the point-light window hack in `outbreakSceneHooks.lightAttenuation` (`games/outbreak/src/shaders.ts:21`) with `openGlassLight` + `openGlassSheen` so house windows cast true rectangular light pools onto porches/lawns and stretch warm rectangular reflections across damp sidewalks, hardwood floors, and the parked car.

### 6. Pre-Fog Silhouette Darkening & Selective Warm-Practical Hue Retention
- **Blog game source:** `src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanPost.ts` (`uLimbo`, `uCharcoal`, `uLimboColor`, `standby`, lines 603–614, 656–674) and `perf/open-plan-fire.md`.
- **How it works:** Darkens non-luminous background surfaces with charcoal ink *before* multiplying by atmospheric transmittance (`color = color * transmittance + fog * (1 - transmittance)`), so dark silhouettes sit *behind* glowing light shafts rather than crushing the air to black; then selectively retains high saturation on warm fire/lamp/LED accents while desaturating cold peripheral scenery.
- **Map to Outbreak:** Apply this composition order to Outbreak's unexplored/remembered room shroud (`games/outbreak/src/shaders.ts:195`): darken unlit room surfaces before adding window/flashlight air shafts, and preserve warm practical/flashlight chroma (`warmBias`) in remembered rooms so explored interiors look atmospheric rather than washed-out grey.

### 7. CPU-Built Spatial Light-List Grid (`uLampGrid`) + Static Lamp Depth Atlas
- **Blog game source:** `src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanLampLighting.ts` (`buildOfficeLampLightingData`, `officeLampSurfaceGlsl`, lines 50–72, 175–271).
- **How it works:** Precomputes a `0.5 m` cell `RGBA8` texture (`uLampGrid`) storing up to 4 candidate lamp indices per cell, plus a static `512×512` radial depth atlas rasterised once on the CPU from the furniture/wall mesh.
- **Map to Outbreak:** Build a `192×192` (`0.5 m` cell) `uLampGrid` for Outbreak's static town lamps so `lightSum` tests at most 4 static lamps per pixel instead of looping over all ~22 frustum-visible lamps, and cache static polar shadow rows across frames when doors are stationary.

### 8. Closed-Form 3D Settled Height Fog Shared Across Surface, Bloom & Air
- **Blog game source:** `src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanAtmosphere.ts` (`officeOpticalDepth`, `officeTransmittance`, lines 161–171) and `openPlanPost.ts` (`brightMaterial`, line 368).
- **How it works:** Integrates vertical exponential haze density analytically along the 3D view ray (`(exp(-s0*k) - exp(-d*k)) / k * exp(-originY / H) + far² * 0.09 * uHazeFar`) and uses that exact transmittance for surface extinction, volumetric segment weights, and pre-attenuating HDR before bloom extraction.
- **Map to Outbreak:** Replace the 2D plan-distance fog in `makeLookSceneShader` (`packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts:1048`) with settled 3D height fog so ground mist pools in low yards, driveways, and street gutters while leaving upper roof ridges and tree crowns crisp.

### 9. Local-Space Triplanar Unlit Wear Maps + Nyquist-Filtered Fibres on Props & Cars
- **Blog game source:** `src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanHardware.ts` (lines 371–420), `openPlanHardwareFinishes.ts` (lines 24–44), and `openPlanServiceMaterial.ts` (`fibres`, `normalVariance`, lines 99–103, 217–219).
- **How it works:** Projects compact unlit wear textures (`floppy-wear.webp`, `casing-wear.webp`) in local coordinates with power-4 triplanar weights to modulate albedo, $F_0$, and roughness together, paired with band-limited procedural weave/brush lines `sin(phase) * exp(-fwidth(phase))` and geometric normal-variance roughness clamping.
- **Map to Outbreak:** Apply triplanar sampling from `materialTexArray` (`packages/kit/textures/`) + `fwidth`-filtered wood/metal/fabric grain to Outbreak's `MAT.timber` and `MAT.lit` furniture (`sofa`, `bed`, `table`, `fridge`, `stove`, `doors`, and the driveway sedan), which currently use flat unmodulated vertex colours.

### 10. Midtone-Masked Dual-Scale Film Grain + Dark-Gated 8-Bit IGN Quantization Dither
- **Blog game source:** `src/articles/opencode-2-0/openPlanPost.ts` (lines 685–703).
- **How it works:** Masks triangular dual-hash film grain to midtones (`smoothstep(0.02, 0.2, outLuma) * (1.0 - smoothstep(0.75, 1.0, outLuma))`) with a boost inside light shafts (`+ 0.018 * beamLift`), and adds 1-ULP Interleaved Gradient Noise dither `(ign(fragCoord.xy) - 0.5) / 255.0 * lit` gated to lit pixels (`luma > 0`) right before 8-bit sRGB output.
- **Map to Outbreak:** Add dark-gated IGN dither and midtone grain masking to `postShader` in `packages/kit/gpu/shaders.ts:945` (and `makeLookPostShader`) to eliminate 8-bit Mach banding on dark twilight streets and smooth practical lamp falloffs while keeping unlit night shadows clean.
